Module 1 · Lesson 1.3

Where Brian Encounters AI Without Realizing It

Everyday Tools That Are Quietly AI

Before Brian ever opens a dedicated "AI tool," he has already used several without noticing. His email inbox filters junk mail into a spam folder by recognising patterns learned from millions of previous emails — a working, everyday example of narrow AI. Shopping and streaming sites use a close cousin of the same idea: a recommendation engine that studies what someone has viewed or bought before, then predicts what else they are likely to want.

None of this involves a dramatic robot moment. It is quiet software, running in the background of tools Brian already trusts, doing exactly the kind of pattern-recognition and prediction work that defines AI.

AI Already Inside Rafiki Insights' Spreadsheets

Rafiki Insights' bread-and-butter work is helping small Eldoret businesses make sense of their numbers — and the spreadsheet tools Brian uses every day for that already lean on AI. Auto-fill features that predict the next values in a column, and built-in forecasting functions that project next month's sales from a client's sales history, are both applying the same pattern-recognition and prediction principles covered earlier in this unit.

Key Point. When Brian pulls up a forecast chart for a client's sales trend, he is already using AI — he doesn't need a separate, dedicated "AI app" to say he has adopted it. Recognising this helps him spot where AI is already earning its keep, instead of assuming it is still off in the future.

Chat Assistants As a Daily Helper

Chat assistants are the most visible AI tool in Brian's week: he might ask one to draft the first version of a client email, summarise a long data report into three bullet points, or explain a data term he's forgotten. Used this way, a chat assistant is simply another narrow AI tool doing a language-based judgement task — drafting and summarising — the same category of work as the pattern-recognition tasks covered in the earlier lessons.

Taken together, spam filters, recommendation engines, spreadsheet forecasts, and chat assistants show that AI at Rafiki Insights isn't a single dramatic system — it's a handful of narrow, specialised tools already woven into an ordinary working day.
