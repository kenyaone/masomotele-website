Laundry and Linen Care

Fabric Care Labels

Care labels specify water temperature, whether tumble drying is safe, and any special handling — following them prevents damaging a client's clothing or delicate items, and matters more for expensive or sentimental items.

Separating Laundry

Soiled items (especially those with bodily fluids) should be washed separately from regular laundry, and colours should be separated to prevent bleeding/staining — basic but important sorting habits.

Linen Change Frequency

A bedridden or incontinent client generally needs bed linen changed more frequently than someone fully mobile and independent — matching frequency to actual need, not a fixed generic schedule, both maintains hygiene and reduces skin issues like pressure sores.

Key Point. Frequency should be based on the client's actual condition and need, not a one-size-fits-all schedule applied to every client identically.

Safe Handling of Soiled Linens

Wearing gloves when handling heavily soiled linen, and avoiding shaking it out (which can spread contaminants into the air), are basic infection-control habits that apply directly to laundry work.
