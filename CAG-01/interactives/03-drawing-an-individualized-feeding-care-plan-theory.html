Drawing an Individualized Feeding Care Plan

What Is an Individualized Feeding Care Plan?

A written plan that turns a client's assessed dietary needs into specific, day-to-day meals, matched to their health conditions, preferences and feeding route. It exists because no single generic menu is safe or suitable for every client.

Types of Individualized Feeding Plans

Plans can be short-term, built for a temporary illness; therapeutic, built around a specific medical condition such as diabetes or a lactose-free diet; or a long-term maintenance plan for ongoing daily care. The type used depends on the dietary needs identified during assessment.

Grocery Lists and Recipes

Once needs are known, Njeri builds a grocery list of ingredients that fit the plan, and selects recipes that use them safely — avoiding known allergens, keeping sugar controlled for a diabetic client, and so on.

Key Point. Every recipe and grocery item should be checked back against the dietary needs identified in the assessment before it goes onto the plan.

Building a Weekly Menu

A menu spreads chosen recipes across the week to keep meals varied and balanced, rather than repeating the same dish every day, while still fitting the client's time, budget, and daily routine.
