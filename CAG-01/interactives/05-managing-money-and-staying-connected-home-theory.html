Module 5 · Lesson 5.5

Managing Money and Staying Connected Home

Basic Budgeting

Planning a simple budget — how much goes to personal living expenses, how much is saved, and how much is sent home as remittances — before the money starts arriving helps avoid the common pattern of spending unpredictably and having little left to show for months of work.

Safe Money Transfer

Using recognised, regulated money transfer services (bank transfers, established mobile money services, licensed remittance companies) rather than informal or unfamiliar channels reduces the risk of lost or stolen money.

Key Point. An unfamiliar "helpful friend" offering to transfer money home for a small fee is a common scam pattern — sticking to recognised, regulated channels protects hard-earned money.

Avoiding Common Scams

Requests for advance fees to "release" a transfer, unsolicited offers of unusually good exchange rates, or pressure to send money through an unfamiliar, unverified channel are common scam patterns targeting migrant workers specifically.

Staying Connected

Regular video calls, voice messages, and simply scheduling consistent times to talk — rather than only reaching out during emergencies or problems — help maintain genuine emotional connection with family despite the physical distance.
