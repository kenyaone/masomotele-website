Visa, Documentation and Travel Requirements

Common Required Documents

Typical requirements include a valid passport (with sufficient remaining validity), the correct work visa for the destination country, a medical clearance certificate, and a police clearance certificate confirming no relevant criminal record.

Validity Periods Matter

A passport close to expiry, or a medical/police clearance issued too far in advance, may be rejected at the border or by the employer — checking specific validity requirements (often "at least 6 months remaining") well before travel avoids a last-minute crisis.

Key Point. Checking document validity weeks before travel, not days before, gives time to actually fix a problem if one is found.

Keeping Copies Separately

Keeping photocopies (or photos) of the passport, visa, and contract stored separately from the originals — and sharing copies with a trusted family member back home — protects Njeri if documents are ever lost or, in a worst case, confiscated inappropriately by an employer.

Only Licensed, Verified Agencies

Working only through a licensed, verifiable recruitment agency (checked against the relevant government labour ministry's official list) significantly reduces the risk of exploitation compared to an informal, unverified "agent".
