Module 6 · Lesson 6.7

Winding Up After First Aid

Documentation

Immediately after an incident, Njeri records the key facts — what happened, when, what first aid was given, and the outcome — while details are still fresh and accurate.

Waste Disposal and Management

Used first aid materials such as soiled dressings or gloves are disposed of safely according to workplace procedure, protecting both Njeri and others from infection risk, and any first aid kit items used are noted for restocking.

Key Point. Contaminated waste is handled with the same care as during the first aid itself — winding up safely is part of the job, not an afterthought.

Report Writing

A first aid report is written factually and clearly: what was observed, what actions were taken, and by whom, avoiding personal opinions or guessed diagnoses, so it can be relied on by medical staff, supervisors, or family afterward.
