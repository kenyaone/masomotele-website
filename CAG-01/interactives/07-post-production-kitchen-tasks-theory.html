Module 7 · Lesson 7.8

Post-Production Kitchen Tasks

Cleaning and Storing Tools and Equipment

After a meal is served, tools, equipment and leftover materials are cleaned and stored properly according to workplace procedure, ready and safe for their next use.

Cleaning Surfaces and Floors

Work surfaces and floors are cleaned to remove food residue and spills, reducing the risk of contamination, pests, or slips for the next person using the kitchen.

Key Point. Post-production cleanup isn't optional once the client has been fed — an unclean kitchen becomes the starting point, and the risk, for the very next meal.

Waste Disposal

Food waste and packaging are disposed of according to workplace procedure, keeping the kitchen hygienic and safe.
