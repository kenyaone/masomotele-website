Module 7 · Lesson 7.6

Previous Preparation in the Kitchen

PPE and the Production Room

Before cooking, Njeri puts on the appropriate PPE, such as an apron, hairnet, and clean footwear, as per manufacturer or workplace instructions, and airs the production room to keep it fresh and free of built-up cooking odours or excess humidity.

Assembling Cleaning Materials and Equipment

Cleaning materials and equipment are selected and assembled before cooking starts, not searched for mid-task, so the kitchen can be kept clean as work progresses rather than only at the end.

Cleaning and Assembling Food Production Tools

All tools, surfaces, and equipment to be used are cleaned and assembled in advance, following workplace procedure — a clean starting point reduces contamination risk throughout meal preparation.

Key Point. Skipping previous preparation to "save time" usually costs more time later, once contamination or missing tools interrupt the actual cooking.

Collecting and Weighing Ingredients

Following the recipe, Njeri collects all needed ingredients and weighs or measures them accurately before starting to cook, which keeps the meal consistent with the client's feeding plan and avoids last-minute substitutions.
