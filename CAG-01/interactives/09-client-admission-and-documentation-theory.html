Module 9 · Lesson 9.3

Client Admission and Documentation

Admission Information

Admission or registration typically gathers: personal/contact details, known medical conditions, current medications, allergies, and emergency contact information — a foundation that shapes safe care decisions going forward.

Why Accuracy Matters

An inaccurate allergy record, a missed medication, or wrong emergency contact information can lead to real harm — documentation is not bureaucratic overhead, it is a safety mechanism that later care decisions depend on.

Key Point. A caregiver who knows a detail that documentation missed (an allergy, a medication change) should always raise it — silence here can have real consequences.

Confidentiality

Client information gathered during admission or ongoing care is confidential — Njeri should not discuss a client's medical details with unrelated third parties, neighbours, or on social media, even with good intentions.

Njeri's Supporting Role

Njeri can help a client recall details or communicate them clearly to facility staff, but she is not the one who officially records or owns that documentation — her role is to support accurate communication, not to replace the facility's own record-keeping process.
