Using Translation and Communication Apps Safely

When Translation Apps Help

For everyday conversation — greetings, simple requests, basic comfort-checking — a translation app can genuinely help bridge a language gap between Njeri and a client or family member who does not share a common language.

Extra Caution for Medical/Legal Content

Machine translation can subtly mistranslate medical or legal terms in ways that matter significantly — a mistranslated dosage instruction or consent explanation carries real risk. For anything genuinely important (medical instructions, legal consent), a human interpreter or the actual facility's translated materials are safer than relying solely on an app.

Key Point. A translation app is generally fine for "would you like some tea?" — it is a much riskier tool for "take this medication twice daily" without a way to double-check accuracy.

Confirming Understanding

After using a translation app for something important, checking that the client or family member actually understood correctly (through their response, or a follow-up question) catches a translation error before it causes a real problem.

Respecting Privacy

Some translation/communication apps may store or process what is typed into them — being mindful about what personal or medical details are entered, especially into free, general-purpose apps, protects client privacy.
