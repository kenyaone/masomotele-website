What AI Tools Can Help Caregivers With

Everyday Helpful Uses

An AI assistant can help Njeri translate a phrase for a client who speaks a different language, draft a clear shift-handover note, or explain an unfamiliar medical term in plain language — genuinely useful, time-saving support for common everyday tasks.

Not a Replacement for Professional Judgement

AI tools are not a substitute for a doctor, nurse, or supervisor's professional medical judgement — asking an AI assistant "should I give this medication now?" and acting purely on its answer would be a serious mistake, since it has no access to the client's actual medical record or current condition.

Key Point. An AI assistant has no access to a specific client's actual medical history or current condition — treating its general answer as a specific medical decision is genuinely risky.

Being Specific

A specific question ("explain dysphagia in simple terms for a caregiver with no medical background") gets a much more useful answer than a vague one ("tell me about health stuff").

Recognising the Limits

A text-based AI tool cannot see the client, cannot examine them, and does not know anything about them unless Njeri explicitly tells it — its answers are general knowledge, not a specific assessment of the actual person in front of her.
