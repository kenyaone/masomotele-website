RAM Troubleshooting and Reseating

Physical practicum required. This step teaches the judgement and correct sequence for a real hands-on repair task. To be certified competent in the physical technique itself (not just the decision-making), you must complete a supervised in-person practicum session — this online step alone does not certify hands-on ability.

1. Common RAM-related symptoms

  • Random Blue Screens of Death, especially with varying error codes each time
  • System fails to POST, sometimes with a RAM-related beep pattern or lit diagnostic LED
  • Random freezes or crashes during memory-heavy tasks
  • System boots but only detects a lower capacity than installed

2. Correct reseating procedure

  1. Power off, unplug, discharge residual power, put on the anti-static wrist strap
  2. Open the RAM slot retention clips on both sides
  3. Fully remove the stick, inspect the gold connector edge for dust or corrosion
  4. Gently clean with a soft anti-static brush if needed, never touch the connector edge with bare fingers
  5. Reinsert firmly and evenly until both retention clips click closed on their own
A stick that is not fully seated is one of the single most common causes of a no-boot or intermittent-crash complaint, and it looks deceptively normal at a glance.

3. Isolating a faulty stick

With multiple sticks installed, test one stick at a time in the same slot: remove all but one, boot, and check stability or run a memory diagnostic. Repeat with each stick individually. If the system is stable with stick A alone but crashes with stick B alone, stick B is the fault, not the slot or the motherboard.

4. Slot pairing for dual-channel

Most motherboards require RAM to be installed in specific slot pairs, usually labelled or colour-coded, to run in dual-channel mode. Installing two sticks in the wrong slots (for example, both in adjacent slots instead of the correct paired slots) still works, but silently drops back to single-channel performance -- always check the motherboard manual rather than guessing.
