Laptop Battery Health and Replacement

Physical practicum required. This step teaches the judgement and correct sequence for a real hands-on repair task. To be certified competent in the physical technique itself (not just the decision-making), you must complete a supervised in-person practicum session — this online step alone does not certify hands-on ability.

1. How batteries degrade

Lithium-based laptop batteries have a limited number of full charge cycles before their maximum capacity noticeably declines, typically losing significant capacity after roughly 300 to 500 full cycles, though this varies by battery chemistry and usage pattern. This is normal, expected wear, not necessarily a fault or defect.

2. Reading a battery health report

Windows can generate a detailed battery report from the command line, showing design capacity versus current full-charge capacity. A battery reporting significantly below its original design capacity, alongside a customer complaint of dramatically reduced runtime, confirms battery wear as the cause rather than something else, such as background software draining power unusually fast.

3. Safe replacement procedure

  1. Power off completely and unplug the charger
  2. For laptops with a removable battery, a simple release latch is often all that is needed
  3. For laptops with an internal, non-removable battery, the bottom panel must be opened, and the battery is often secured with a small number of screws plus adhesive strips requiring careful, patient removal
  4. Follow standard ESD precautions throughout, exactly as covered earlier in this course
Damaged lithium batteries can be a genuine fire risk. A battery that is visibly swollen or puffy should be handled with extra care and disposed of properly, never punctured or forced.

4. Charging habits and battery life

Consistently keeping a battery at 100 percent charge while constantly plugged in, or regularly draining it fully to 0 percent, both accelerate long-term wear compared to keeping it in a more moderate charge range where practical. This is useful, genuinely actionable advice to share with customers who want to maximise the life of a new replacement battery.
