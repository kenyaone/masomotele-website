What AI Assistants Can Help Diagnose

1. A genuinely useful starting point

AI assistants are strong at quickly organising a shortlist of plausible causes for a described symptom, especially for an unfamiliar error message, beep pattern, or an uncommon combination of symptoms Peter has not personally seen before. This does not replace the systematic troubleshooting process from earlier in this course, it feeds directly into step 2 of it: establishing a theory of probable cause.

2. Describing a symptom clearly

A vague prompt like "computer broken" produces a vague, unhelpful answer. A specific description, for example the exact error message text, when it happens, what changed recently, and what has already been tried, produces a genuinely more useful, targeted response.

3. Explaining unfamiliar signals

An AI assistant can be genuinely useful for quickly explaining an unfamiliar error code, beep pattern, or diagnostic LED combination, especially for a less common motherboard brand whose manual is not immediately at hand -- effectively acting as a fast-access reference tool.

4. A starting point, not a final answer

AI-suggested causes are theories, not confirmed diagnoses. Step 3 of the troubleshooting process from earlier in this course, testing the theory, still applies in full -- an AI suggestion should be verified with an actual check, exactly like any other theory, before parts are ordered or replaced.
