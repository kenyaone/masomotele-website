Using AI to Look Up Error Codes and Parts

1. Interpreting error codes quickly

Modern systems generate a huge range of error codes, from Windows Stop codes to specific hardware diagnostic codes. Rather than manually searching through lengthy documentation, describing the exact code to an AI assistant can quickly summarise what it generally indicates and common associated causes, saving real research time on a busy day at the shop.

2. Checking part compatibility

Before ordering a replacement RAM stick, PSU, or CPU, describing the exact existing system specifications, such as motherboard model, socket type, and current RAM specifications, to an AI assistant can help quickly narrow down compatible options worth investigating further.

3. Comparing similar-looking parts

Two RAM sticks or two power supplies can look nearly identical while having meaningfully different real-world specifications. AI can help summarise and compare key specifications side by side, saving time manually cross-referencing multiple datasheets or product pages.

4. Always confirm against the actual manufacturer specification

AI can be confidently wrong about compatibility. Before actually ordering a part, the final compatibility check should always be confirmed against the motherboard manufacturer's own official specification page or manual, not taken purely on an AI assistant's word -- getting this wrong wastes both money and the customer's time.
