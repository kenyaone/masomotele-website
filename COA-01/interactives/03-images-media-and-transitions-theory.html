Images, Media and Transitions

Images on a Slide

Insert → Pictures places an image; corner handles resize it proportionally, same as in Word. Picture Format tools let you crop, add a border, or apply a quick style. Images should be reasonably high resolution — a blurry, stretched photo undermines an otherwise professional deck.

Video and Trimming

Insert → Video adds a clip that can play directly on a slide. Playback → Trim Video lets you cut a long clip down to just the relevant seconds, without needing separate video-editing software.

Key Point. Trim a video to only the relevant section before presenting — sitting through a whole untrimmed clip wastes a live audience's time.

Transitions

A Transition (Transitions tab) is the animated effect as one slide changes to the next (Fade, Push, Wipe, etc.). A single subtle transition (like Fade) applied consistently looks professional; a different flashy transition on every slide looks distracting and amateurish.

Key Point. One consistent, subtle transition throughout the deck beats a different flashy one on every slide.
