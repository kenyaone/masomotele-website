Interface and Slide Basics

Views

Normal view is the default — a large editing area for one slide, with a thumbnail strip on the left. Slide Sorter view shows every slide as a small thumbnail grid, ideal for reordering slides by dragging them, or spotting one that is out of place in a long deck.

Key Point. Use Slide Sorter view whenever you need to reorder or get an overview of many slides at once — dragging one slide in Normal view is slower and error-prone.

Slide Layouts

Each slide uses a layout — a pre-arranged set of placeholder boxes (Title Slide, Title and Content, Two Content, Blank, etc.). Home → Layout lets you change it after the slide exists. Picking the right layout up front (e.g. Title Slide for slide 1, Title and Content for the rest) keeps a deck visually consistent.

Adding and Managing Slides

New Slide (Home tab) adds a blank slide with a chosen layout. Duplicate Slide (right-click a thumbnail) copies an existing slide exactly — useful when the next slide needs a very similar layout. Delete removes a slide entirely; it cannot be undone once the file is closed and reopened, so undo (Ctrl+Z) immediately if it was a mistake.
