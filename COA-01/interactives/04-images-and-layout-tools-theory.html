Images and Layout Tools

Alignment Guides

Publisher shows temporary pink/blue guide lines as you drag an object near the edge or centre of another object or the page, "snapping" it into perfect alignment. This replaces guessing pixel positions by eye.

Key Point. Watch for the snap guides while dragging — they are the fastest way to line objects up perfectly without a ruler.

Aligning Multiple Objects

Selecting several objects at once (Shift-click each one, or drag a selection box around them) and using Arrange → Align lets you align them all to the left, centre, or evenly space them — useful for lining up three price boxes evenly across a page.

Layering and Grouping

When objects overlap, Bring Forward/Backward (right-click) controls which sits on top — e.g. text needs to sit in front of a background colour box, not hidden behind it. Group (select multiple objects → right-click → Group) locks several objects together so dragging one moves them all — useful once a logo-plus-caption combination is finalised and should move as one unit.

Key Point. Group finished object combinations before final positioning — it prevents accidentally dragging just one piece out of place later.
