Module 4 · Lesson 4.3

Templates for Flyers and Brochures

Customising a Template

Built-in templates use placeholder text ("Your Business Name Here") and generic stock photos. Clicking a placeholder selects it for immediate replacement with real content — the layout, spacing, and design stay intact as long as you replace rather than delete-and-rebuild.

Colour and Font Schemes

Page Design → Colour Schemes and Font Schemes let you swap a template's entire palette or typography in one click, similar to a Theme in PowerPoint — keeping the layout but applying Amani Stationers' own brand colours consistently across every text/shape in the design.

Key Point. Changing the Colour Scheme once updates every matching design element — far faster than manually recolouring each shape.

Replacing Images

Right-clicking a placeholder photo and choosing Change Picture swaps it for a real photo while keeping the exact size, position, and crop frame the template designed around — dragging a new photo in from scratch risks breaking that careful layout.

Saving as a Reusable Template

Once customised with the shop's branding, Save As → Publisher Template saves it as a reusable starting point for every future flyer, the same way a Word Template works — future flyers start from this branded version instead of a generic one.
