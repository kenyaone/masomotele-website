AI-Assisted Formulas and Data Analysis in Excel

Describing a Task in Plain Language

Instead of knowing the exact function name, Brenda can describe what she needs — "a formula that shows the total sales for July only, from a column of dates and a column of amounts" — and an AI assistant can suggest an appropriate formula (e.g. a SUMIFS-style approach), which she then adapts to her actual column letters.

Checking the Suggestion

An AI-suggested formula is built around example cell references, not necessarily Brenda's real ones. It must be checked and adjusted to match her actual sheet's column letters and row ranges before it will work correctly — pasting it in unchanged risks referencing the wrong data entirely.

Key Point. An AI-suggested formula is a starting template, not a guaranteed drop-in answer — always confirm it points at your real ranges.

Explaining Existing Formulas

AI is also useful in reverse — pasting in an unfamiliar formula Kevin inherited from a previous assistant and asking "what does this do?" can get a plain-language explanation, helping understand a spreadsheet built by someone else.
