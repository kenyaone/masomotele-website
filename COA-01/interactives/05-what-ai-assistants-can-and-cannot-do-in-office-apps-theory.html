What AI Assistants Can and Cannot Do in Office Apps

Where AI Shows Up

Modern versions of Word, Excel, and PowerPoint increasingly include built-in AI assistants (like Microsoft Copilot) or work alongside separate AI chat tools (like ChatGPT) that a user copies text to and from. Either way, the AI is a drafting assistant, not an autonomous decision-maker — a human still directs and approves everything it produces.

What AI Is Genuinely Good At

AI tools are strong at: drafting a first version of a letter or email, summarising a long document into key points, suggesting an Excel formula for a described task, and proposing slide layouts or talking points. These save real time on the "blank page" problem.

Key Point. Think of AI as a fast first-draft generator — useful for getting started, not for final, unchecked output.

What AI Often Gets Wrong

AI tools can produce confident-sounding but incorrect specific numbers, dates, prices, or local facts about Eldoret suppliers it has no real knowledge of — this is sometimes called "hallucination". It can also misunderstand exactly what a formula needs to reference. AI has no access to Amani Stationers' actual private stock figures unless Brenda explicitly provides them.

Key Point. AI does not know Amani Stationers' real numbers unless Brenda types them in — it will still confidently guess if asked, and that guess can be wrong.
