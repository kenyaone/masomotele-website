Windows and Multitasking

The Three Window Buttons

Every window's top-right corner usually has three buttons: Minimise (shrinks it to the Taskbar without closing it), Maximise (expands it to fill the whole screen), and Close (an X — shuts the program/window down entirely, prompting to save unsaved work first if needed).

Key Point. Minimise and Close look similar but do very different things — Minimise keeps the program running out of sight; Close ends it.

Resizing and Moving

Dragging a window's title bar (the top strip) moves it around the screen. Dragging an edge or corner resizes it, letting Faith see two windows partially side by side if needed.

Switching and Snapping

Holding Alt and pressing Tab cycles through all open windows quickly, without using the mouse. Dragging a window to the left or right edge of the screen often "snaps" it to fill exactly half the screen — a fast way to view a form and a reference document side by side.
