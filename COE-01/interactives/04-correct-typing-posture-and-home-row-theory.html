Module 4 · Lesson 4.1

Correct Typing Posture and Home Row

Posture

Sitting with a straight back, both feet flat on the floor, and the screen roughly at eye level reduces strain during long typing sessions — important for someone typing forms all day at a front desk.

The Home Row

The home row is the middle row of letter keys — left hand rests on A S D F, right hand on J K L ;. Every finger has a "home" key it returns to between key presses, which is the foundation of typing without constantly looking down.

Key Point. The small raised bumps felt on the F and J keys let a typist find the home row by touch alone, without looking.

Why This Matters

Constantly looking down at the keyboard to find each letter is slow and breaks concentration on what is actually being typed. Learning the home row by feel, even slowly at first, is what eventually allows faster, more accurate typing without watching the keys.
