Mouse Skills: Click, Drag and Scroll

Single Click vs Double-Click

A single left-click selects an item (highlighting it) without opening it. A double-click (two quick clicks) opens a file or launches a program. Confusing the two is a very common early mistake — a slow double attempt sometimes registers as two separate single clicks instead.

Right-Click

Right-clicking an item opens a context menu — a list of relevant actions for that specific item (Rename, Delete, Copy, Properties, etc.), different depending on what was right-clicked.

Key Point. Right-click is one of the fastest ways to discover what actions are even possible on a file, folder, or piece of text.

Drag and Scroll

Click-and-drag (holding the left button down while moving the mouse) can select a range of text, move a file, or resize a window, depending on where the drag starts. The scroll wheel (the small wheel between the buttons) moves up and down a long page or document without needing the on-screen scroll bar.
