Touchpad Gestures on a Laptop

Basic Movement and Clicking

Moving one finger across the touchpad moves the on-screen pointer, the same as moving a mouse. Many laptops support tap-to-click — briefly tapping the touchpad once acts like a mouse's left-click, instead of physically pressing a button.

Two-Finger Gestures

Sliding two fingers up or down the touchpad typically scrolls a page, the touchpad equivalent of a mouse's scroll wheel. Tapping with two fingers at once often performs a right-click, opening a context menu.

Key Point. Two-finger gestures on a touchpad map directly onto scroll wheel and right-click actions from a regular mouse.

Pinch to Zoom

On many laptops, placing two fingers together and spreading them apart on the touchpad zooms in on a photo or webpage; pinching them together zooms back out — a gesture borrowed from touchscreen phones.
