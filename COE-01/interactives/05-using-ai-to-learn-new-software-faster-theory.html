Module 5 · Lesson 5.2

Using AI to Learn New Software Faster

"How Do I...?" Questions

Instead of feeling stuck on an unfamiliar program, Faith can ask an AI assistant something like "how do I insert a table in Microsoft Word?" and get a clear, step-by-step explanation in plain language — similar to asking a patient colleague to walk her through it.

Following Up

If the first answer is confusing or does not quite match what Faith is seeing on her screen, she can simply ask a follow-up question — "I don't see that button, what else could it be called?" — the same way a real conversation would continue, rather than assuming the first answer was final or complete.

Key Point. Treat it like an ongoing conversation, not a one-shot answer — asking a clarifying follow-up is normal and often necessary.

Practising, Not Just Reading

Reading an explanation of how to do something is different from actually doing it. Faith should try the steps on her own computer right after asking, while the explanation is fresh, rather than only reading about it and never actually practising.
