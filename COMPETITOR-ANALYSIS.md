**Strengths:**
- Government accredited (established 2011)
- Offers both short courses & diplomas
- Full campus in Eldoret
- Diverse course offerings (Engineering, Business, IT, Hospitality)
- Lower fees for diplomas (Ksh 15,000-18,000/term)
- Brand recognition as "technical institute"

**Weaknesses:**
- ❌ Focus on 2-3 year diplomas (slower entry to market)
- ❌ Limited online/flexible options
- ❌ Outdated website & digital presence

- ❌ Less marketing/SEO visibility

**Your Advantage:**
✅ Shorter courses (1-2 months vs 2-3 years)  
✅ Flexible payment plans  
✅ Modern digital marketing  
✅ Faster course updates  
✅ Better online presence  
✅ Specialized expertise vs generalist

**Threat Level:** HIGH - They're the government institution (trusted brand)
