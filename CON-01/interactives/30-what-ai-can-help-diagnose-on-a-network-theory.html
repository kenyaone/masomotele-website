What AI Can Help Diagnose on a Network

1. A genuinely useful starting point

AI assistants are strong at quickly organising a shortlist of plausible causes for a described network symptom, especially something unusual James has not personally seen before. This feeds directly into step 2 of the structured process from earlier in this course: establishing a theory of probable cause -- it does not replace steps 3-6.

2. Describing a network symptom clearly

A vague prompt like "network broken" produces a vague, unhelpful answer. A specific description -- which devices are affected, what tests have already been run (ping, ipconfig results), what changed recently -- produces a genuinely more useful, targeted response, exactly the same principle covered for hardware diagnosis in this course\'s sister course on computer repair.

3. AI as a starting point, not a final answer

AI-suggested causes are theories, not confirmed diagnoses. Step 3 of the troubleshooting process, testing the theory, still applies in full -- an AI suggestion for a network issue should be verified with an actual ping, ipconfig check, or cable test, exactly like any other theory, before any fix is applied or any part is ordered.
