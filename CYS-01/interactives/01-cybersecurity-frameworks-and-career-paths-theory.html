Cybersecurity Frameworks and Career Paths

What a Framework Is

A security framework is a structured, widely-recognised set of best practices and processes — rather than inventing security practices from scratch, an organisation can align with an established framework as a starting foundation.

NIST Cybersecurity Framework

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework organises security activities into core functions: Identify (know your assets/risks), Protect (safeguards), Detect (spot incidents), Respond (act on incidents), and Recover (restore normal operations). It is widely referenced across industries, including finance.

Key Point. Even a smaller company like SafariPay benefits from a framework — it provides a structured checklist rather than guessing what "good security" should look like.

Career Paths

Cybersecurity careers span many specialisations: SOC analyst (monitoring for incidents), penetration tester (authorised attack simulation), security engineer (building defensive systems), GRC analyst (governance, risk, and compliance), and more — this course touches on several of these areas across its units.
