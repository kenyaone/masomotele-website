The CIA Triad and Security Goals

Confidentiality

Confidentiality means only authorised people can see sensitive data — a customer's mobile money balance, a password, a transaction history. Encryption, access controls, and need-to-know permissions all serve confidentiality.

Integrity

Integrity means data is accurate and has not been tampered with — a transaction amount cannot be silently changed in transit, a customer's balance cannot be secretly edited. Checksums, digital signatures, and audit logs help detect or prevent unauthorised changes.

Availability

Availability means systems are usable when legitimately needed — SafariPay's mobile money app being down during peak hours is an availability failure, even if no data was stolen or altered. Backups, redundant servers, and defending against denial-of-service attacks all protect availability.

Key Point. The three goals can pull against each other — very strict confidentiality controls (constant re-authentication) can hurt availability (frustrated, locked-out users) — real security work balances all three.
