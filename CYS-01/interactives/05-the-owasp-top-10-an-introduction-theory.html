The OWASP Top 10: An Introduction

What OWASP Is

OWASP (Open Web Application Security Project) is a nonprofit that publishes widely-used, freely available web application security resources. Its Top 10 list ranks the most critical, common web application security risks based on real-world data and expert consensus.

Why It Changes Over Time

The Top 10 is periodically revised as attack patterns, technology, and real-world incident data evolve — a risk that was top-ranked a decade ago may be reprioritised as defences improve or new risks emerge. Treating an old version as permanently authoritative misses this evolution.

Key Point. The specific rankings shift version to version, but the underlying discipline — know your most likely risks, prioritise defending against them — stays constant.

Using It to Prioritise

With limited time and budget, Grace cannot fix every theoretical risk equally. The Top 10 gives a data-informed starting point for prioritising security review and remediation effort on SafariPay's web-facing systems, rather than guessing.
