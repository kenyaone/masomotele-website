## 🔧 Advanced Settings

### **Location Targeting**
```
Primary: Kenya (all regions)
Secondary: East Africa (Uganda, Tanzania)
Exclude: None

Rationale: MTTI serves all Kenya. Regional expansion opportunity.
```

### **Device Targeting**
```
Mobile: 100% (normal bid)
Desktop: 100% (normal bid)  
Tablet: 80% (reduce bid 20%)

Rationale: Mobile users search more, desktop has higher conversion.
```

### **Time of Day Bidding**
```
Weekdays 9-5 PM: +20% bid (working hours, job seekers active)
Weeknights 6-10 PM: Normal bid (people researching)
Weekends: -10% bid (lower intent)
Early morning 5-8 AM: Normal bid (job seekers researching)

Rationale: Target high-intent searchers during decision time.
```

### **Negative Keywords (Block These)**
```
- free
- youtube
- reddit
- how to teach
- jobs hiring (unless CNA-specific)
- salary only (no course interest)

Rationale: Prevent clicks from people not interested in training.
```

## 📞 Conversion Tracking Setup

### **What to Track:**
1. **Inquiry Form Submission** (Goal: 4% of clicks)
2. **Course Enrollment** (Goal: 1-2% of clicks)
3. **Phone Call** (if applicable)
4. **WhatsApp Contact** (high intent)

### **How to Set Up:**
```
1. Go to Google Ads → Tools & Settings → Conversions
2. Create "Inquiry Form" conversion
3. Copy tracking code to your form
4. Create "Course Enrollment" conversion
5. Assign values: Inquiry = KES 1,000, Enrollment = KES 12,000+
6. View conversion data in campaign reports
```

## 🎓 Pro Tips

1. **Start with CNA campaign first** (highest ROI potential)
2. **Pause low performers after 200 clicks** (no point burning budget)
3. **Increase winning keyword bids by 20%** monthly
4. **A/B test headlines** (rotate winners)
5. **Run campaigns year-round** (January/September peak hiring)
6. **Use call extensions** (click-to-call on mobile)
7. **Add location extensions** (show MTTI address in ads)
8. **Monitor competitor ads** (use SEMrush/Ahrefs free version)

## 📱 Important: Mobile Optimization

**Your landing pages MUST be mobile-optimized because:**
- 60%+ of searches on mobile
- Slow mobile pages = 70% bounce rate
- Google penalizes non-mobile-friendly pages

**Test your pages:**
1. Go to: https://pagespeed.web.dev/
2. Enter your landing page URLs
3. Fix any "poor" performance issues
4. Target: Score 75+

---

## 🎯 Success Metrics

**Campaign is working if:**
- ✅ CTR > 2% (ads are relevant)
- ✅ Landing page bounce < 50% (page is good)
- ✅ Conversion rate > 4% (good quality leads)
- ✅ Cost per lead < KES 5,000 (profitable)
- ✅ 30-50 clicks/week (enough volume)

**Pause campaign if:**
- ❌ CTR < 1% (change ad copy)
- ❌ Conversion rate < 1% (change landing page)
- ❌ Cost per lead > KES 10,000 (not profitable)
- ❌ Quality score < 4 (relevance issue)
