Vector Shapes, the Bezier Tool and Node Editing — Practical

Every complex vector illustration — even one built with the Bezier tool — is really just a stack of individually editable objects, and that's the idea to practice here for Ivy's next Kerio Fresh Juice asset. Draw three different shape types on the canvas: a rectangle, an ellipse, and a line. Then switch to the Select tool, click on one of them, and use the Properties panel to change its fill color — the same select-and-edit move Ivy relies on once a Bezier path is built from individual nodes.
