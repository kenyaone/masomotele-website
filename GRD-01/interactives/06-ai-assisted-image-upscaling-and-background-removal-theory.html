AI-Assisted Image Upscaling and Background Removal

Why Client Files Are Often Low-Quality

A common real scenario at a small print and branding shop: a client only has a tiny logo saved years ago from Facebook, or a low-resolution screenshot, or an old scanned business card. Blown up for a shop sign or large flyer, these files look blurry and pixelated — not usable as-is.

What AI Upscaling Really Does

AI upscaling tools use pattern recognition to estimate and fill in plausible extra detail — they do not truly recover original data that was never captured. This is genuinely useful for cleaning up photos. But for a logo that needs to be crisp and scale to any size, the safest practice is still to trace it into clean vector shapes in CorelDraw after upscaling, rather than deliver the upscaled bitmap itself as the “final” scalable logo.

AI Background Removal for Photos

AI background removal tools quickly isolate a product or portrait subject for flyers, social posts, and brochures, saving real time over manual selection. The catch: fine details like hair, fur, or semi-transparent edges can come out ragged or clipped, so Ivy always checks the edges closely and touches up any rough spots before using the result.

Key Point. AI upscaling and background removal save real time on repetitive image cleanup — but for anything that must be perfectly crisp (a logo) or perfectly clean-edged (client-facing print), always review the AI output closely and finish the job as proper vector or cleaned artwork in CorelDraw, not the raw AI output.
