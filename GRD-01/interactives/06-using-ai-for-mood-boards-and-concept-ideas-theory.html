Using AI for Mood Boards and Concept Ideas

What a Mood Board Is (and Why AI Speeds It Up)

A mood board is a quick collection of reference images, colours, and textures that guide the direction of a design before any real artwork is built. Designers used to cut these from magazines or browse stock photo sites for hours. Today, an AI image generator can produce several concept variations from a short text prompt in seconds — a fast way for Ivy to explore colour palettes, textures, and stylistic directions before she ever opens CorelDraw.

Writing a Useful Concept Prompt

A good AI prompt for concept exploration includes the subject or industry, the mood (a few descriptive adjectives), a colour palette, and a style reference. A vague prompt like cafe logo produces generic, unfocused results. A detailed prompt like minimal modern logo concept for an eco-friendly cafe, warm green and cream tones, hand-drawn leaf motif, flat vector style gives the AI tool enough direction to produce genuinely useful mood board ideas.

From AI Concept to CorelDraw Artwork

AI-generated images are pixel-based, often inconsistent up close, and their exact origin and licensing are unclear. Ivy treats them purely as a mood board: she picks the colours, motifs, and overall style direction she likes, then builds the real logo herself as clean, original vector shapes in CorelDraw — the file the client actually receives.

Key Point. An AI-generated mood board is a starting point for ideas and direction — never trace it directly or hand a client an AI-generated image as the “final logo.”
