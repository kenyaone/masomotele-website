Building Blocks -- Prefixes, Roots and Suffixes

1. The pattern behind medical words

Most medical terms are built from three types of pieces: a prefix (comes before, often shows location, number or time), a root (the core meaning, usually a body part or system), and a suffix (comes after, often shows a condition or procedure). Once you recognise the pieces, you can often work out an unfamiliar word without memorising it as a whole.
