2. Why "normal" is a range

Normal vital signs vary between individuals, and even for one person, throughout the day. This is exactly why a single reading is checked against a RANGE, not one exact number -- and why noticing a significant change from a patient\'s own usual baseline can matter just as much as comparing against the general population range.

3. What an abnormal reading means for Mercy

An abnormal reading is a reporting trigger, not a diagnosis. If a reading falls outside the normal range, Mercy\'s job is to double-check the reading was taken correctly, record it accurately, and report it to a nurse promptly -- not to interpret what it means clinically or decide on any treatment.
