Safe Battery Replacement and Disposal

Spotting a Swollen or Damaged Battery

Signs that a battery has swollen or is otherwise damaged include the back cover or screen edges lifting slightly and no longer sitting flush, a visible bulge or curve in what should be a flat battery casing, the phone feeling unusually warm even when idle, or a battery that drains or charges strangely compared to before. Purity checks for these signs before even starting a repair, since a swollen battery changes how carefully the rest of the disassembly needs to be done — any extra pressure applied near it becomes risky.

Removing It Safely

A swollen lithium battery is under internal pressure and can catch fire or vent harmful chemicals if it is punctured, bent sharply, or crushed. Purity never uses a metal pry tool directly against the surface of a swollen battery, and never digs underneath it forcefully. Instead she uses proper adhesive-release pull tabs where available, and where the adhesive resists, she applies a small amount of isopropyl alcohol along the edges to soften it and waits for it to loosen rather than pulling harder. If a battery still will not release with gentle, patient effort, she stops and treats it as a case to escalate rather than force out.

Key Point. A swollen battery is a fire risk. Never puncture, bend, or force it during removal. If it resists gently, stop — do not add more pressure. Loosen adhesive with isopropyl alcohol and patience instead.

Disposing of It Properly

Once removed, a used or damaged lithium battery is hazardous e-waste, not ordinary trash. Throwing it in a regular bin risks it being crushed or punctured later in the waste stream, which can start a fire. At QuickFix Phone Repairs, Purity places removed batteries in a dedicated, non-flammable storage container kept specifically for this purpose, away from other supplies, until they can be taken to a proper battery or e-waste recycling point. Treating this step as part of the job, not an afterthought, protects the shop, her colleagues, and the environment.
