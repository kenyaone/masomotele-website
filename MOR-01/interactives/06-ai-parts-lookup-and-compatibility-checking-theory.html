AI Parts-Lookup and Compatibility Checking

Why Phone Variants Matter

Two phones sold under the exact same model name can still have different internal hardware depending on region, carrier, or production batch — a different screen flex connector, a different battery shape, or a different charging port board. At QuickFix Phone Repairs, fitting a part that matches the model name but not the exact variant can mean a part that doesn't connect properly, or worse, damages the board when forced into place.

Asking AI the Right Compatibility Question

To get a useful answer, Purity gives the AI assistant precise details: the exact model number (found in Settings or on the box), and the exact part number of the replacement screen or component she is considering. A prompt like "Is screen part number XYZ-100 compatible with a Samsung Galaxy A12 model SM-A125F?" gives the AI something concrete to reason about, instead of a generic "does this screen fit a Samsung phone."

Key Point. Treat the AI's compatibility answer as a fast first check — always confirm against a supplier spec sheet or the physical part before committing the customer's money.

Cross-Checking the Answer Before Ordering

Sometimes the AI answers confidently but hedges on a specific regional variant, or simply isn't aware of a very new part number. Before placing an order, Purity cross-checks the AI's answer against the supplier's own spec sheet or product listing, and where possible compares the connector type and pin count against the part already in her hand. This extra step catches the cases where AI's first-pass answer would have led to an expensive, wrong order.
