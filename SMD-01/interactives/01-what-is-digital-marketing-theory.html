2. The definition

Digital marketing is every marketing activity that uses electronic devices or the internet to reach customers. It promotes brands, products, or services through digital channels — search engines, social media, email, video, websites — and it meets customers exactly where they already spend their time online.

Two things to notice in that definition:
  1. Digital marketing is not one thing — it is many activities working together.
  2. It meets customers where they already are — you don't force them to come to you.

3. The 9 core elements

Every trainee must know these nine. Click each card to reveal what it means and how Sarah might use it.

1

Content Marketing

Attract with valuable content.
Creating articles, videos, recipes, or posts that help your audience — without a direct sales pitch. Sarah: a weekly "Lunch of the Week" post with the recipe.
2

Social Media Marketing

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X.
Regular organic posts, stories, reels, community engagement on the platforms your customers already use. Sarah: daily lunch photos on Instagram and Facebook.
3

Email Marketing

Targeted messages to subscribers.
Newsletter, weekly menu, promotions, birthday offers — sent to people who consented to hear from you. Sarah: a Sunday menu email to her 30 subscribers.
4

Mobile Marketing

SMS, WhatsApp, mobile apps.
Over 80% of Kenyan internet traffic is on phones — this includes WhatsApp Business, SMS bulk campaigns, and push notifications. Sarah: WhatsApp broadcast to today's regulars.
5

Affiliate Marketing

Partners paid on results.
You pay a commission when a partner brings you a sale. Sarah: pay a KES 100 commission to a Kapsoya boda-boda rider for every new dinner customer he refers.
6

Search Engine Marketing (SEM)

Paid Google adverts.
Google Ads that show at the top of search results for chosen keywords — you pay per click. Sarah: paid ads for "lunch delivery Eldoret CBD" during weekdays 11am–1pm.
7

Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)

Free ranking in Google.
Improving your website so it appears in unpaid Google results — takes weeks to months but keeps working for free. Sarah: ranking her website for "vegetarian lunch Elgon View".
8

Display Advertising

Banners on other websites.
Image or video ads that appear on other sites via the Google Display Network or Meta Audience Network. Sarah: banner on the Nation website targeted at Eldoret readers.
9

Analytics

Measure what's working.
Google Analytics 4, Meta Insights, and platform dashboards — the numbers that tell you what to keep doing and what to stop. Sarah: weekly review of which post drove the most WhatsApp orders.

5. Advantages vs limitations — the honest picture

Digital marketing is not magic. Every good marketer sees both sides.

✓ Advantages

  • Cost-effective — reach thousands for a few hundred shillings.
  • Trackable — you see exact clicks, views, orders.
  • Larger demographic reach — beyond your town.
  • Higher engagement — likes, comments, shares, DMs.
  • Mobile-friendly — critical in Kenya (80%+ mobile).

✗ Limitations

  • Fewer face-to-face moments — relationships feel less personal.
  • Can feel obtrusive — nobody enjoys a pop-up.
  • Needs constant management — one post is not a strategy.
  • Not for every product — a rural elder without a phone won't see it.
  • Ad fatigue — audiences tune out repetitive creative.

6. Kenya legal considerations — the DPA 2019

Digital marketing in Kenya is regulated. The Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 gives every customer rights over how their personal data is collected and used. Get this wrong and you face fines of up to KES 5 million — plus the loss of your customers' trust.

1. Consent

You must obtain consent before sending marketing messages (email, SMS, WhatsApp).

2. Privacy

Customer data must be stored securely. Only collect what you actually need.

3. No spam

Every marketing message must have a clear way to opt out.

4. Copyright

You cannot use other people's images or music without a licence.

5. Truth in advertising

Claims about a product must be accurate. No fake reviews.

6. Data breach reporting

If customer data leaks, you must report within 72 hours to the ODPC.

Regulator to know: the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) — the body that enforces the DPA 2019 and imposes fines.

8. Self-check quiz

Eight questions. Pick an answer and it locks in — you'll see instant feedback.

1. Which is NOT one of the 9 elements of digital marketing?

Correct — print is a traditional (offline) channel. Digital marketing runs on electronic devices and the internet.

2. What percentage of Kenyan internet traffic is on mobile?

Correct — over 80%. That's why mobile-friendly design is not optional for Kenyan businesses.

3. What is the maximum fine under the Kenya DPA 2019?

Correct — up to KES 5 million per breach. Plus reputational damage.

4. Which is a goal — not an element — of digital marketing?

Correct — lead generation is what you're trying to ACHIEVE. SEO, email, and content are HOW you achieve it.

5. Sarah wants dinner deliveries this month. Which two goals matter most?

Correct — dinner delivery is a direct-response product; sales is the immediate goal, and awareness in her three delivery neighbourhoods drives repeat orders.

6. What's the difference between SEM and SEO?

Correct — Search Engine Marketing = paid. Search Engine Optimisation = organic/unpaid.

7. Which advantage matters MOST for a Kenyan small business?

Correct — the cost + mobile combination is why digital marketing is transformative for Kenyan SMEs.

8. Before Sarah sends her first WhatsApp marketing broadcast, what MUST she do?

Correct — the DPA 2019 requires consent and an easy way to opt out for any marketing message.
