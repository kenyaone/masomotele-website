1. Sarah's TikTok flop

Sarah's cousin says she must join TikTok because "a friend's samosa business went viral." Sarah tries — shaky 8-second clip of her kitchen — 12 views. She feels foolish.

The single most important social skill: choosing the right platform for the right audience. Wrong platform = 40 followers a year. Right platform = 40,000.

4. Sarah's 3-platform focus

For Sarah — office workers 25–50 + families in Eldoret — the answer is three:

  • Facebook — her existing 340 friends live here. Lunch menu posts work.
  • Instagram — beautiful food photos generate engagement.
  • Google Business Profile — not "social" but where "lunch delivery Eldoret" searchers actively look.
Not for Sarah (yet): LinkedIn (B2B), Pinterest (wrong demo), X (news-driven). TikTok maybe — only if she can produce one strong video/week.

5. The 4 elements of a campaign

1. Choose platforms

Match to audience, done in §4.

2. Create native content

Facebook post ≠ IG Reel ≠ LinkedIn article. Don't copy-paste.

3. Track

Reach, engagement, clicks, conversions in each platform's Insights.

4. Evaluate + adjust

After 2–3 weeks: what works, what to kill.

6. The 3 professional habits

  • 1. Consistency — post on a schedule. Weekly minimum, daily better.
  • 2. Quality > quantity — one well-photographed lunch beats 10 shaky videos.
  • 3. Engagement — reply to every comment, especially negative, within 24h.
Social media is a conversation, not a broadcast. Broadcasters get ignored.
