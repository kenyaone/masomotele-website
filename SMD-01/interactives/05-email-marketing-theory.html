1. Why email still wins

Three reasons every year for the last decade:

  • Direct ownership — Facebook can change its algorithm, TikTok can be banned. Your email list belongs to you.
  • Measurable — every email tells you exactly who opened, who clicked, who unsubscribed.
  • Best ROI — every credible survey shows KES 30–40 return per KES 1 spent. No other channel comes close.
Sarah's overlooked list: her WhatsApp Business is full of customer phone numbers — many of them gave her an email to receive receipts. She has a list. She just hasn't built it yet.

6. DPA 2019 — consent form builder

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019, marketing emails need explicit opt-in consent. Build Sarah's website subscribe form correctly.

Get the weekly menu, delivered

Anti-pattern: Do NOT pre-tick the consent box. Do NOT assume having someone's phone number means you can email them. Do NOT buy a list — ever. Both are DPA 2019 violations.
