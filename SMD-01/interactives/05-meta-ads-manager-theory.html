1. The 3-level campaign hierarchy

Every Meta advertising campaign is built at three levels. Getting this wrong is the #1 beginner mistake — objectives at the wrong level break the whole campaign.

LEVEL 1

Campaign — WHAT you want

Set your objective (Sales, Traffic, Leads, etc.) and optionally the campaign-wide budget.

LEVEL 2

Ad Set — WHO, WHERE, WHEN, HOW MUCH

Audience targeting, placements, schedule, and bid strategy live here. One campaign can have many ad sets.

LEVEL 3

Ad — THE CREATIVE

The image or video, headline, primary text, and call-to-action button that the user actually sees.

Image / video Headline CTA button Destination URL
Common mistake: Putting the audience on the Campaign level (there is no such setting) or duplicating campaigns instead of adding a second Ad Set. If you want to A/B test two audiences, keep ONE campaign, create TWO ad sets.

2. The 6 ODAX objectives

In 2022 Meta consolidated 11 legacy objectives into six under Outcome-Driven Ad Experiences (ODAX). Pick the objective that matches your business outcome — not the one that "sounds impressive".

A

Awareness

Impressions, reach, ad recall. For brand launches and top-of-funnel visibility.

T

Traffic

Link clicks or landing-page views. For driving people to a website or WhatsApp.

E

Engagement

Likes, comments, shares, video views. For community building.

L

Leads

Instant Forms on Meta or website form fills. For B2B and service leads.

P

App Promotion

App installs and in-app events. For mobile app growth only.

S

Sales

Purchases and conversions. Requires Pixel + CAPI. The workhorse for e-commerce.

Watch out: Pick Traffic when you actually want Sales and Meta will optimise for CLICKERS — not BUYERS. You'll pay for lots of curious visitors and few purchases.

3. The 3 audience types

Click each tab to explore.

Built from Meta's own data

Layer up to five signals to describe who you want to reach:

  • Location — country, city, or a radius pin.
  • Age — 13 to 65+.
  • Gender — all, women, men.
  • Detailed targeting — interests, behaviours, demographics.
  • Language — for reaching a specific language community.
Sarah's Core Audience: Women 25–50, 5 km radius around Kapsoya, Elgon View, and Racecourse, interests: food + family + cooking.

Built from data YOU provide

Six ways to build a Custom Audience:

  1. Customer list — hashed CSV of emails/phones from your CRM.
  2. Website — Meta Pixel-tracked visitors.
  3. App activity — installs or events (needs Meta SDK).
  4. Engagement — people who liked your Page, watched a video, opened a form.
  5. Offline events — matched purchases from a POS.
  6. Catalogue — people who interacted with your product catalogue.
Sarah's Custom Audience: Upload the CSV of her 30 Mailchimp subscribers. Meta matches them to Facebook profiles.

New people who resemble your best ones

Meta finds users who share signals with a source audience — usually a Custom Audience of your best buyers.

6. Budget and the Learning Phase

Meta budgets are either daily (simpler for beginners) or lifetime. For Sarah: KES 400/day × 14 days = KES 5,600 — well inside her KES 9,000 Meta allocation.

The Learning Phase

New ad sets need about 50 conversion events per week for Meta's algorithm to figure out who converts. Below 50, results are inconsistent. Above 50, they stabilise.

0–50 events → Learning
50+ events → Exited Learning (stable)
Do NOT judge a new campaign until it exits the Learning Phase. Kill it too early and you kill a campaign that would have worked. Wait 5–7 days minimum before making changes.

8. Self-check quiz

Ten questions. Pick and lock — you'll see instant feedback when you mark.

1. What are the three levels of a Meta campaign, in order?

Correct — Campaign holds the objective, Ad Set holds audience/placement/budget, Ad holds the creative.

2. Where do you set the audience?

Correct — audience, placement, schedule, and budget all live at Ad Set.

3. Which objective should Sarah pick to sell dinners tonight?

Correct — Sales optimises for Purchase events. Traffic only optimises for CLICKS.

4. Which is a Custom Audience source?

Correct — Pixel-tracked website visitors are a common Custom Audience source.

5. What is a Lookalike Audience?

Correct — Meta finds new users who share signals with your source audience.

6. What triggers "Exited Learning Phase"?

Correct — ~50 events/week is the threshold Meta gives publicly.

7. Which placement setting is safest for a beginner?

Correct — Advantage+ lets Meta shift budget to the best-performing placements automatically.

8. Best ad format to show a 4-item menu?

Correct — Carousel supports 2–10 swipeable cards, one per menu item.

9. Sarah has KES 400/day. When can she judge results?

Correct — judging too early is the #1 waste of budget on Meta.

10. For a local business in Eldoret, best location setting is…

Correct — a radius pin around each delivery neighbourhood focuses spend on people who can actually order.
