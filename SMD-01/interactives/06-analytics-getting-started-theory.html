1. The one rule

Sarah has spent KES 45,000 on Meta Ads over 3 months. She thinks the ads are losing money. Her cousin says switch them off. Sarah is about to make an expensive decision based on a feeling.

The one rule of all digital marketing: you cannot improve what you do not measure. Not "should not". Cannot. Without data, every decision is a guess.

3. A/B testing — the 3 rules

  • Rule 1: Change only ONE thing at a time. Two headlines + two images = you can't tell what caused the difference.
  • Rule 2: Get statistical significance. Professional threshold: ≥ 100 conversions per variant. Below that = noise.
  • Rule 3: Define your winning metric BEFORE the test starts. Deciding after = cherry-picking = dishonest.

5. The reporting cadence

Reporting is not something you do when a client asks. It is a rhythm.

🗓 Weekly
Every Friday · 30 min

Review last 7 days of every active campaign. Winning ad? What to replace next week?

📊 Monthly
Last Friday of month · 90 min

Full month vs goals. Reallocate budget across platforms based on Cost per Result.

🎯 Quarterly
Every 90 days

Strategic review of the quarter. Adjust strategy, drop losers, double down on winners.
