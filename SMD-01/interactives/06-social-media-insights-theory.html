2. The 5 platform tools

  • Meta Business Suite Insights — Facebook AND Instagram. Sarah's main dashboard.
  • X Analytics — free for any X account.
  • LinkedIn Analytics — free for Business Page owners.
  • TikTok Analytics — free in every Business account.
  • Hootsuite — paid, multi-platform. Only worth it if you manage 3+ accounts across 2+ platforms.
For most Kenyan SMEs, the built-in free tools are enough.

5. 3 common mistakes

  1. Measuring too short a period. One bad post in a week ≠ broken strategy. Look at a full month.
  2. Comparing to unrelated benchmarks. Sarah's KES 40k budget ≠ Safaricom's. Compare to yourself, MoM.
  3. Fixating on follower count. Vanity metric. Total sales beats total followers every time.

6. The 80/20 content rule

Every review ends with the same conclusion:

80% engagement
from 20% of posts

Your job: identify the 20%, understand why they work, produce more.

Sarah's top 20% share 3 features: all Reels · all show her face · all end with a clear "Order Now" CTA. That's her formula. She stops experimenting randomly.
