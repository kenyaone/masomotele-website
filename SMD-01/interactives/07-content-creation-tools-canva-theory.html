1. What Canva actually is

Free, browser-based design tool for non-designers. It works by templates. Pick "Instagram Square Post" → drag your photo in → type text → download. 2 minutes. Zero design skill.

  • 250,000+ free templates
  • 1 million+ free photos
  • Unlimited exports
The free tier is enough to run a serious Kenyan SME for years without paying a shilling.

3. Free vs Pro

✓ Free tier

  • 250k+ templates
  • 1M+ free photos
  • Unlimited exports
  • Basic Brand Kit

Cost: KES 0

★ Canva Pro

  • Full Brand Kit (colours, fonts, logos)
  • Magic Resize (1 design → many sizes)
  • Background Remover
  • 100M+ premium images/elements

Cost: ~KES 1,500/mo. Worth it if you make 3+ graphics/week.

4. The 6 alternatives

Adobe Express

Adobe's Canva. Integrates with PS.

Vista Create

Formerly Crello. Strong on animation.

Photo editing + collage focus.

Piktochart

Infographic specialist. Business reports.

Quick social graphics. Simple.

Figma

Collaborative. UI/app design. Advanced.

For 95% of Kenyan SME work: Canva is the answer. Learn Canva first, others later.

6. 3 time-savers · 4 mistakes

Time-savers (save 2 hours/week)

  1. Duplicate last week's design + change the text (never start blank)
  2. Canva's built-in scheduler posts directly to Meta
  3. Magic Resize once → get Instagram feed + Story + Facebook post

Common mistakes

  1. Too many fonts — max 2–3 per design
  2. Every colour — stick to Brand Kit only
  3. Cramming text — max 15 words per image
  4. Ignoring safe zone — 50 px from every edge minimum
