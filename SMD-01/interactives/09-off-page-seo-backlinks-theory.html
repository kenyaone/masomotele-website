1. Why backlinks are votes

When website A links to website B, it's A saying "B is worth reading." That's a vote of confidence. The more high-quality sites vote for you, the more Google trusts you.

Quality > quantity. One link from Nation or Standard is worth more than 100 links from unknown blogs.

2. Dofollow vs Nofollow

✓ Dofollow (what you want)

Passes ranking value. Real websites, news articles, blog rolls.

~ Nofollow

Google ignores for ranking. Facebook, Instagram, comment sections default to nofollow.

Beginner rule: nofollow still brings traffic + awareness, but focus outreach on dofollow opportunities.

6. 5 things NEVER to do

Google Penguin catches every one. Penalties are severe.

1. Buy backlinks in bulk

Guaranteed penalty.

2. Use private blog networks (PBNs)

Google detects the pattern.

3. Stuff exact-match anchor text

"lunch delivery Eldoret" everywhere = spam.

4. Spam comment links

Every SEO cowboy tries. Never works.

5. Join link farms / exchanges

"I'll link you if you link me" schemes.

Recovery from a penalty takes months of formal reconsideration requests. Never risk it.

7. Measurement + Disavow

Two free tools:

  • Google Search Console — Links report — every external site linking to you, weekly
  • Ahrefs Free Backlink Checker — snapshot of any site's top backlinks, no account needed

Weekly 5-min check: are backlinks growing? Are they quality (journalism/blogs) or spam (casino/adult)?

Disavow tool tells Google to ignore specific bad links. Use sparingly — don't accidentally block real backlinks that just look small.
