1. The 7 on-page elements

Every page Google reads has seven signals. Miss any one and your ranking suffers. Fix all seven and Sarah's home page competes for the money keyword.

2. Element 1 — SEO title

The clickable headline in Google results and the browser tab.

  • Length: 50–60 characters. Google cuts off after ~60.
  • Keyword first — highest weight at the start.
  • Brand last — separated by a dash or pipe.
  • Compelling — promise something specific.
Sarah's home page title: Lunch Delivery Eldoret — Karibu Kitchen (52 chars). Keyword first. Brand last. ✓

3. Element 2 — URL / slug

  • Hyphens between words, never underscores
  • Lowercase only
  • Descriptive, not random codes or dates
  • Includes the keyword
❌ Bad✓ Good
karibukitchen.co.ke/?p=127karibukitchen.co.ke/
karibukitchen.co.ke/Lunch_Menu_2024/karibukitchen.co.ke/menu
karibukitchen.co.ke/cat=3&id=7karibukitchen.co.ke/delivery

4. Element 3 — meta description

  • Length: 120–160 characters
  • Include the keyword naturally
  • End with a call to action
Sarah's: Fresh lunch delivered to Eldoret CBD in 30 minutes. Order by 12, eat by 1. Menu changes daily. WhatsApp us on 0782 XXX XXX. (148 chars) ✓

5. Elements 4 & 5 — heading structure

Every page has ONE H1. Under it: H2s for main sections. Under H2s: H3s for sub-sections. Correct hierarchy is not optional.

# H1: Fresh lunch, delivered to your desk.
## H2: Today's specials
## H2: Where we deliver
## H2: How to order
### H3: By WhatsApp
### H3: By phone
### H3: In person

8. Self-check quiz

Ten questions. Answer AFTER you've got every Yoast dot green.

1. Recommended SEO title length?

Correct — Google cuts off around 60 characters.

2. Where does the target keyword go in the SEO title?

Correct — keywords at the start of the title carry the most weight.

3. Recommended meta description length?

Correct — 120–160 chars fits without being cut off.

4. How many H1s per page?

Correct — one H1 only. Use H2/H3 for hierarchy.

5. Which is a good URL slug?

Correct — hyphens, lowercase, descriptive, contains the keyword.

6. Alt text serves…

Correct — dual purpose. Both matter.

7. Which alt text is best for Sarah's ugali+stew hero image?

Correct — descriptive, natural, includes location keyword.

8. Minimum body words for a Home page (SEO target)?

Correct — 300 words for a Home page; 1,000+ for a blog post.

9. Keyword density target?

Correct — 3–5 naturally distributed mentions. More = keyword stuffing = penalty.

10. Yoast shows all green dots. What does that mean?

Correct — green = the on-page foundations are correct. Ranking also depends on 9.4 (technical) and 9.5 (backlinks).
