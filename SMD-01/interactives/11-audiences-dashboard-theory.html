1. Three audience families

Meta's Audiences screen has three tabs and every real campaign uses all three:

  • Custom — people who already touched Karibu Kitchen. Warmest.
  • Lookalike — new people who resemble a Custom source. Meta's most powerful automation.
  • Saved — reusable Core-audience recipes (location + age + interest). Coldest.

3. Lookalike creator — try it above

Open the Lookalike Audiences tab. Pick a source, drag the similarity slider — the estimated reach updates in real time. Notice the reach jumps from ~180k (1%) to ~1.8M (10%) — bigger reach, lower similarity.

Kenya DPA 2019 — before you upload:
  • You must have documented consent from every person on the list.
  • The uploader hashes PII with SHA-256 on your machine — never send raw email/phone to any third party.
  • You must honour deletion requests within 30 days — delete the row and re-upload.
Break these and the ODPC can fine up to KES 5 million.

5. Saved Audiences

Click the Saved Audiences tab. These are Core-audience recipes — location + demographics + interests — you keep on the shelf for repeat campaigns. Nothing personal about the users, just filters.
