1. The six creative formats

Meta consolidated ad creative into six formats. Every campaign uses at least two.

  • Single image / video — workhorse. Fast to test.
  • Carousel — 2–10 swipeable cards. Multi-item stories.
  • Collection — hero + product grid → Instant Experience. E-commerce.
  • Reels — 9:16 vertical video, 15–60s. Gen Z winner.
  • Stories — 9:16 vertical, 15s, ephemeral. Urgency & drops.

3. Character counters & safe zones

Meta truncates copy at fixed lengths. Overshoot and your ad shows "…See More" or clips your headline. The counters above turn amber near the limit and red past it.

  • Primary text — 125 chars before mobile "See More"
  • Headline — 40 chars (bold under image)
  • Description — 30 chars (small print, under headline)
Vertical safe zone on Reels & Stories: keep every important element in the middle 80% of the frame. Top 10% is covered by the profile chip. Bottom 10% by the CTA button.

4. CTA button dropdown

Meta ships ~12 CTA options — the five most useful for Kenyan SMEs are in the dropdown. Match the CTA to the destination:

  • Shop Now → e-commerce product page
  • Order Now → cart / WhatsApp order flow
  • Learn More → blog / long-read / brand story
  • Sign Up → lead form / newsletter
  • Message → Messenger / WhatsApp Business chat
Sarah's pick: Order Now → wa.me link with the menu item pre-filled. One tap and the customer is already writing to her.
