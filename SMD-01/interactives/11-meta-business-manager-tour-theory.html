1. Personal ad account vs Business Manager

Sarah of Karibu Kitchen Ltd ran her first campaign in Lesson 5.4 on the personal ad account Meta auto-creates behind every profile. It worked — but it is fragile. If her personal Facebook is disabled, her ads die with it. Her cousin cannot help without logging in as Sarah.

Business Manager (rebranded Meta Business Suite) is a separate container that OWNS the Page, ad account, Pixel, Instagram, and catalogue. Sarah is an Admin — but she is not the container. The business itself is.

The rule of five: ownership, delegation, audit, tracking, spending limits. If any one of these matters to your business — set up Business Manager on day one.

3. The Add People flow

Click Users in the sidebar above, then + Add People. Enter an email, pick a role, and Meta sends an invitation link. The person accepts through their own Facebook profile — nobody shares passwords.

Why email > profile lookup: the person can accept from any Facebook profile they own. Their access is tied to that profile, not to the email address.

4. The 6 permission roles — click each to reveal

Meta ships six standard roles. Pick the tightest role that lets the person do their job.

Role permission explorer

Rule of thumb: only the owner and one deputy should ever be Admin. Everyone else — Employee, plus task-based sliders on each asset.

5. Ad Account panel with pending invitations

Click Accounts in the sidebar of the mock above. You'll see three pending invitations — Sarah must Resend (nudge) or Revoke. Long-pending invites are a common security hole.

Revoke pending invites older than 7 days. Attackers hunt for stale invitation links.

6. Data Sources, Brand Safety & Integrations

Three tabs you will visit often once you're at scale:

  • Data Sources — where the Pixel and CAPI live (Lesson 11.2).
  • Brand Safety — block lists so your ad never runs next to content you would not sponsor.
  • Integrations — WhatsApp Business, WooCommerce, Shopify. Revoke any app you no longer use.

7. Role-picker challenge (auto-marked)

Sarah needs to grant access to four new people. Pick the correct role for each. You'll be scored out of 4.

Q1. Sarah's cousin Jane will post daily photos to the Karibu Kitchen Page but should NOT touch billing.
