5. Automatic vs Manual (Cost Cap) bidding

Two bid strategies. Ninety per cent of Brian's ad groups stay on Automatic forever.

AttributeAutomatic OptimisationCost Cap (manual)
What Brian setsDaily budget onlyMax bid per conversion
Learning PhaseEnters cleanly, exits at ~50 eventsOften stalls — TikTok throttles to hit ceiling

Best usedEvery new ad group. Default. Winner for most experienced advertisers too.ONLY after 50+ conversions and CPA has stabilised, and only when you must protect an exact CPA.
Brian's ratio~90% of ad groups, forever~10%, and only late-stage
Cost Cap looks like control but usually starves the ad group of impressions during Learning. Result: an ad group that "cannot spend" and stays flat. Automatic delivers. Trust it.

6. Attribution window sanity check

Recap from 12.1. Brian's default is 7-day click + 1-day view. Every review, he compares the two.

  • If 7-day click and 1-day view show similar conversion totals — view attribution is genuinely adding value.
  • If 1-day view dominates 7-day click — TikTok is claiming credit for people who would have bought anyway. Red flag.
Ad Group7-day click purchases1-day view purchasesSignal

Real incrementality lives in click-through, not view-through. Trust the click.

7. Self-check quiz

1. What is the Hook Rate?

Correct — Hook Rate measures whether the first 2 seconds stop the swipe. Above 30% is strong.

2. Brian's target ROAS is…

Correct — 3× is Brian's target. Below 0.5× triggers a kill.

3. TikTok's healthy CTR floor is…

Correct — 1% floor, 3%+ is strong. Below 1% and the creative isn't earning clicks.

4. Which bidding strategy does Brian use for 90% of ad groups?

Correct — Automatic. Cost Cap starves ad groups during Learning.

5. How often does Brian run the kill/scale/leave loop?

Correct — every 3 days. Faster and you kill campaigns that would have won. Slower and you burn budget on losers.

6. When scaling a winner, Brian raises budget by…

Correct — 20% is the safe scale increment. Larger jumps re-enter Learning and blow up CPA.

7. Why does Brian save custom columns as "Brian pro" once and reuse?

Correct — the point of the saved layout is comparability week over week.

8. In the attribution sanity check, what is a red flag?

Correct — 1-day view dominance suggests TikTok is claiming credit for people who would have bought anyway. Real incrementality lives in click-through.
