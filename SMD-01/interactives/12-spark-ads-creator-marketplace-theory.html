1. What Spark Ads are — deep view

A Spark Ad takes an existing organic TikTok video and promotes it as a paid ad. It keeps the original video URL, likes, comments, shares, and every profile visit routes back to the poster.

TikTok's own data: Spark Ads deliver +130% CTR and +134% completion rate vs traditional produced ads. Because they don't look like ads — they ARE organic content, just paid to reach more people.

Two source options:

  • Brian's own Chapa Fitness videos. No authorisation code needed — the account is already a Business Centre asset from 12.1.
  • A creator's video. Requires the creator's explicit authorisation via a Video Code.

3. Authorisation code flow

To turn a creator's video into a Spark Ad, TikTok requires the creator's explicit permission via a Video Code — a short alphanumeric string valid for 7 or 60 days.

Video Code generated

60-day validity code appears.

4
Brian pastes into Ads Manager

The video becomes a promotable Spark Ad.

Live demo: Wanjiku hits "Generate code" — a 60-day authorisation appears. Brian pastes it into Ads Manager → Ad → Identity → Use TikTok post → Enter code.

Wanjiku's authorisation code: — tap generate —

The video keeps her handle, her likes, her comments, and every profile-click routes back to Wanjiku. Brian pays only for reach.

Codes expire. 7-day is common for one-off tests. 60-day is standard for Spark rights inside a creator brief. Renew before it lapses or the ad stops delivering.

5. The 6-field professional creator brief

Every deal starts with a brief. Six fields. No exceptions.

FieldChapa Fitness example
1. The ask"One TikTok video, 30–45 seconds, review of Chapa Fitness training shorts."
2. Deliverable countOne draft plus one round of revisions.
3. Hook requirementFirst 3 seconds must show the product physically.
4. Usage rights60-day Spark Ads authorisation via Video Code.
5. Budget in KES"KES 35,000 plus Spark rights."
6. LegalKenyan tax invoice with KRA PIN.
Ambiguous briefs kill deals. Creators overpromise, quality drops, Spark rights get forgotten. A tight brief is the professional's protection — and the creator's too.

6. Whitelisting vs Spark Ads — when to use each

Both let brands run ads under a creator's identity. They are NOT the same.

Spark Ads (start here)

  • Promotes a specific existing video the creator posted
  • Setup: creator generates Video Code → brand pastes into Ads Manager
  • Keeps original likes, comments, engagement
  • Time-boxed: 7 or 60 days
  • Lightweight legally — one video, one code
  • All three of Brian's Nov 2026 deals are Spark Ads

Whitelisting (heavier)

  • Creator gives the brand right to run ads from the creator's own handle for any video, including videos the creator has not personally posted
  • Setup: TikTok Ads Manager → Identity settings → Creator Handle
  • Powerful for scale but heavier legally — the creator lends their identity to campaigns they may not have posted
  • Ongoing arrangement, usually 3–6 months minimum
  • Reserve for creators you have worked with 6+ months
The rule: Spark Ads first. Whitelisting only after a creator has already delivered three successful Spark videos and you trust their brand voice enough to speak for you.
