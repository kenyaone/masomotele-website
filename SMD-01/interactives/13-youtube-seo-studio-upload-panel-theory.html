    2. Thumbnail — the biggest lever after title

    Thumbnails carry more ranking weight than tags, description, or chapters. Only the title matters more.

    1. Upload at 1280 × 720, 16:9, under 2 MB.
    2. High-contrast face or hero object. Face-forward thumbnails beat text-only on average.
    3. On-screen text no more than 4-5 words. Legible on a phone in bright sunlight.
    4. Brand colour consistency across every thumbnail in the channel. Sarah's is maroon + cream.
    The sunlight test: If Sarah cannot read the thumbnail text on her phone stepping outside her Eldoret kitchen at noon, she rewrites it.

    3. Chapters — first must be 0:00

    YouTube auto-parses chapter markers from the description. It requires three rules or it silently ignores them:

    • First timestamp must be exactly 0:00
    • Minimum three chapters
    • Each chapter at least 10 seconds long
    Common bug: creators start chapters at 0:05 or 0:12. YouTube then drops chapter parsing entirely. Sarah's video starts at 0:00 ✓.

    4. Real-time SEO Score panel

    The SEO Score in the top-right of the Studio mock updates as you edit fields on the left. Scroll back up and try:

    • Delete the title → watch the score drop to red.
    • Change the title to My video (no keyword) → still red, different reason.
    • Delete tags → yellow.
    • Restore the description → green.
    Green does not mean best. It means every checklist item is present. Great SEO on YouTube also needs a strong hook in the first 8 seconds and a thumbnail that stops the scroll.
