3. NAP consistency checker — 4 Kenyan directories

NAP = Name, Address, Phone. Must be exactly identical across every property. One missing full stop breaks Google's trust match. Below, Karibu Kitchen appears on 4 directories. Mark each row as consistent or inconsistent.

4. The 15-minute Monday morning routine

Every Monday, 15 minutes in the Performance report. Four checks. Same discipline as the Search Console routine from Lesson 9.6.

Check 1 — Are searches growing?

Week-over-week total searches trending up = good. Flat means optimisation stalled.

Check 2 — Is Discovery % rising?

Discovery is the new-customer engine. Rising % = more new eyeballs each week.

Check 3 — Interactions vs searches?

Action rate up = listing more convincing. Down = attributes or photos missing.

Check 4 — Any new reviews?

Reply within 48 hours. Every review response is read by every future customer.
