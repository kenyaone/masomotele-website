Animated Text and Sticker Overlays

Entrance and Exit Animations

CapCut and Canva Video both offer a set of preset text animations. Fade In/Out is the smooth, safe default — text gently appears and disappears with no distraction. Rise Up moves text up into position from below, adding a bit of energy while staying easy to read. Typewriter reveals letters one at a time, which suits a short punchy tagline but is risky for anything with numbers, since the full text isn't actually visible until the animation finishes. Bounce and Spin are playful and eye-catching, best suited to short stylish words or stickers rather than dense information.

Timing for Readability

A useful rule of thumb: give text enough hang time that a viewer could read it out loud, comfortably, roughly twice over. A call-to-action like a phone number needs to sit fully static on screen — not still animating in or out — for at least about 3 seconds, so someone scrolling their feed can actually read it, or pause to screenshot it.

Position matters just as much as timing. CapCut and Canva Video both show safe-zone guides for vertical video: text placed in the bottom-third safe zone is a common choice, but it still needs to sit clear of the platform's own UI — caption text, like/comment/share icons, and username overlays that Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts draw near the very bottom edge.

Stickers, Overlays and PIP

Beyond text, CapCut and Canva Video support stickers and overlay/PIP (picture-in-picture) elements — logo bugs, order-now graphics, social handles — layered on top of the main video. These lightweight elements can afford punchier, shorter-lived animation since they aren't carrying critical information the viewer must read carefully.

The key distinction is what the overlay is actually communicating. Something like a small "Follow us!" sticker is a quick brand moment — it can bounce in and out in about a second. A phone number or address is different: getting it wrong costs the client a customer, so it deserves the calmer, longer-held treatment.

Key Point. For a CTA like "Order Now! 📞 0712 345 678," prioritize readability over flair: use a calm entrance (Fade In or Rise Up, not Typewriter), hold the full text static for at least 3 seconds before exiting, and place it in the bottom-third safe zone clear of platform icons. Save punchier Bounce/Spin animations and shorter on-screen duration for lightweight stickers that don't need to be read carefully.
