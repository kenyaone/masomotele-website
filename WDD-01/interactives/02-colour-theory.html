Colour

Colour Formats

CSS colours can be named (red), hex (#dc3545), or specified as RGB values (rgb(220, 53, 69)) — hex and RGB give precise, reusable control that named colours cannot.

Contrast Matters

Text needs sufficient contrast against its background to be readable — pale grey text on a white background may look stylish but can genuinely fail to be readable for many visitors, an accessibility concern, not just a style preference.

Key Point. A colour contrast checker tool can confirm whether a text/background pairing actually meets accessibility guidelines, rather than relying on how it looks on one particular screen.

Consistent Colour Use

Defining a small, consistent palette (a primary brand colour, a couple of accents, and neutral greys) and reusing it throughout a site looks more professional than colours chosen ad hoc per page.
