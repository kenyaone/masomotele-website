Transitions and Animations

CSS Transitions

transition smoothly animates a CSS property change over time — instead of a button's colour instantly snapping on hover, transition: background-color 0.2s makes it fade smoothly, feeling more polished.

What to Animate

Properties like transform and opacity animate smoothly and efficiently in most browsers; animating properties like width or top directly can feel less smooth (janky) since they force the browser to recalculate layout on every frame.

Key Point. Preferring transform/opacity for animation, over properties that trigger layout recalculation, is a real, measurable performance difference, not just a style preference.

Using Motion Purposefully

Subtle, quick transitions (0.15-0.3 seconds) generally feel responsive and polished; animations that are too slow or too frequent can feel sluggish or distracting rather than helpful.
