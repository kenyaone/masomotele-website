Module 4 · Lesson 4.6

Going Live

Pre-Launch Checklist

Before a site goes live: test every link, check the site on both a phone and a desktop screen, verify forms actually submit correctly, and proofread all text — small mistakes are far more visible to real visitors than to the person who has been staring at the code for days.

Uploading the Final Files

Once tested, the final HTML/CSS/JS/image files are uploaded to the live hosting account's public_html folder (via cPanel's File Manager or FTP) — this is the actual moment a site becomes visible to the public at its domain.

After Launch

Launch is not the end — checking the live site works correctly for real visitors, monitoring for broken links or errors, and being ready to make small fixes afterward are all part of responsibly running a real website, not a one-time task.

Key Point. A site that "worked on my computer" during development can still reveal real issues only once it is live on the actual domain — always do a final check after upload, not just before.
