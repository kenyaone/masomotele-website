Performance and PageSpeed

Why Speed Matters

A slow-loading page loses visitors before they even see the content — page speed directly affects both user experience and, for many search engines, how highly a page ranks in results.

Common Performance Wins

Compressing images before uploading them, minimizing the number of separate CSS/JS files a page requests, and avoiding unnecessarily large fonts or scripts are all straightforward, high-impact ways to speed up a typical small business site.

Key Point. Oversized, uncompressed images are, in practice, the single most common cause of a slow-loading small business website.

Measuring, Not Guessing

Tools like Google PageSpeed Insights measure a real page's load performance and point to specific, concrete fixes — treating page speed as something to actually measure, rather than assume, catches real problems a quick visual check would miss.
