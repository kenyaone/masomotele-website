═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
NOTES FOR IMPLEMENTATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

QUALITY OVER QUANTITY:
- 5 high-quality backlinks from DA 40+ sites beat 20 low-quality links
- Focus on relevance (education + Kenya + tech training)
- Avoid paid link schemes (violates Google guidelines)

NATURAL LINK BUILDING:
- Great content attracts natural backlinks
- Success stories and unique data (like earnings data) get shared
- Build relationships, not just links

LINK ANCHOR TEXT:
- Use natural anchors: "MTTI Eldoret", "computer training Kenya",
  "TVETA-accredited courses"
- Avoid over-optimization (all links shouldn't be "best computer course")
- Mix branded and keyword-focused anchors
