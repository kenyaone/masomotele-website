The Market Opportunity in Kenya 2026

Why Now?

  • Growing Tech Adoption: More laptops, desktops, printers in homes and businesses
  • Limited Competition: Most repair shops are unqualified, unreliable, or expensive
  • High Demand: "Where can I find a good repair person?" — common question
  • Recurring Revenue: Same customers return 2-3x/year
  • Premium Services: People pay well for trustworthy repairs

Eldoret City Alone:

  • ~10,000+ computers in use (homes, offices, schools, internet cafes)

If you capture just 1% of this market: KES 30,000-75,000/month. Realistic is 5-10%: KES 150,000-500,000+/month.

Skills You Need

Non-Negotiable Skills

  • Hardware Assembly: Know every component, how to remove/install
  • Basic Troubleshooting: Diagnose common problems
  • OS Installation: Windows, Linux, basic setup
  • Component Testing: Identify faulty parts

Nice-to-Have Skills

  • Laptop repair (more profitable than desktops)
  • Data recovery (premium service, KES 5,000-20,000/job)
  • Network troubleshooting
  • Printer repair (easy add-on service)

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • ❌ Not getting certified: Customers want trustworthy technicians. Training matters.
  • ❌ Underpricing: Charge fairly. You're providing a service, not doing charity.
  • ❌ No warranty: Offer 7-14 day warranty on repairs. Builds trust.
  • ❌ Slow communication: Reply within 1 hour on WhatsApp. Speed matters.
  • ❌ No receipts/documentation: Keep records. Professional = more customers.
  • ❌ Lack of specialization: "I fix computers" vs "I specialize in data recovery" — be specific.
