How to Start a Computer Repair Business in Kenya

From KES 50,000 startup to KES 300,000+ monthly revenue

Why Computer Repair is a Goldmine Business Opportunity

Every business and home in Kenya has computers. When they break, people panic. And they'll pay good money for fast fixes. This is why computer repair is one of the most profitable businesses to start with minimal capital.

The beauty? You can start from your home with just KES 50,000, and scale to a full shop with employees within a year.

Before you take a single customer, you need real skills. Trying to learn from YouTube videos while customers wait is a recipe for bad reviews and lost income.

  • Soldering & component repair (fixing motherboards)

Step 3: Pricing Strategy (The Key to Profit)

Don't undercharge. New repair technicians often charge KES 500-1,000 per job, which is leaving money on the table.

Recommended pricing structure for Kenya market:

  • Basic diagnostics: KES 2,000 (identify the problem)
  • Software issues (virus removal, OS install): KES 3,000-5,000
  • Hardware repairs (RAM upgrade, HDD replacement): KES 5,000-10,000
  • Soldering/motherboard repair: KES 15,000-30,000 (premium service)
  • Data recovery: KES 10,000-20,000
Pro tip: Most customers have no idea what's wrong with their computer. Charge for diagnostics first (KES 2,000), which gets applied to the repair cost if they go forward. This filters out tire-kickers and gets you paid even for jobs you don't complete.

Step 4: Marketing & Customer Acquisition

Phase 1 (Month 1): Free/Low-Cost Marketing

  • Tell everyone you know (friends, family, neighbors)
  • Post on WhatsApp status with before/after repair photos
  • Join local Eldoret community Facebook groups
  • Offer a referral bonus (KES 500 for each customer they refer)

Phase 2 (Month 2-3): Paid Channels

  • Google Ads targeting "computer repair near me"
  • Facebook/Instagram ads to locals (budget: KES 2,000-5,000/month)
  • WhatsApp business with quick response time

Revenue Projections (Year 1)

Month 1-2: 3-5 customers/week at average KES 5,000 = KES 60,000-100,000/month

Month 3-6: Build reputation, 10-15 customers/week at average KES 6,000 (higher rates as you gain skill) = KES 240,000-360,000/month

Month 7-12: Established business, 20+ customers/week = KES 400,000-600,000/month

Year 1 Total Revenue: KES 2,500,000 - 4,000,000

Year 1 Net Profit (after costs): KES 2,000,000 - 3,500,000

That's a 5,000% ROI on your KES 40,000 initial investment in Year 1. Not bad for a business you can run from your home.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Undercharging: Value your time. You're solving critical problems.
  • No warranty: Offer 30-day warranty on parts to build trust.
  • Poor communication: Update customers daily. They worry about their data.
  • No inventory: Stock common parts. Fast turnaround = higher prices.
  • Ignoring the business side: Track expenses, save profit, reinvest wisely.

Scaling to a Full Shop

After 6-12 months of successful operation, you can:

  • Rent a small shop (KES 10,000-20,000/month)
  • Hire 1-2 technicians (KES 15,000-20,000 each)
  • Expand to offer other services: phone repair, printer repair, networking setup
  • Target business clients (faster payment, higher contract values)
