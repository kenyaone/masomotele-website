🔥 High Demand Easy

What you'll do: Design websites using WordPress, Wix, or Figma. Help small businesses get online.

Training needed: 4-6 weeks

💰 KES 30,000-100,000 per project

How to start: Learn WordPress basics → build 3 practice sites → post portfolio on Fiverr/Upwork → get first client

2. Digital Marketing Freelancer

🔥 High Demand Easy

What you'll do: Social media management, content creation, Facebook ads for small businesses.

Training needed: 2-4 weeks

💰 KES 10,000-50,000/month per client (x 3-5 clients = KES 30,000-250,000)

How to start: Learn Facebook Ads + Instagram basics → manage 1 business as portfolio → pitch to others

3. Video Editor

🔥 High Demand Easy

What you'll do: Edit TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube videos for content creators and businesses.

Training needed: 3-4 weeks (lots of free tutorials)

💰 KES 5,000-30,000 per video

4. Computer Repair Technician

🔥 High Demand Medium

What you'll do: Repair computers, laptops, install software. Work from home or small shop.

Training needed: 4-8 weeks

💰 KES 20,000-80,000/month (scalable with marketing)

How to start: Get certified → buy basic tools → start from home → scale to shop

Course at MTTI: Computer Repair & Maintenance (2 months, KES 15,000)

5. Content Writer / Blogger

💼 Medium Demand Easy

What you'll do: Write blog posts, articles for websites. Ghost write content.

Training needed: 1-2 weeks (mostly practice)

💰 KES 5,000-50,000 per article

How to start: Write 5 sample articles → post portfolio on Medium/personal blog → pitch on Fiverr/LinkedIn

Tip: No formal training needed. Start writing.

6. Graphic Designer

🔥 High Demand Medium

What you'll do: Design logos, social media graphics, flyers for businesses.

Training needed: 3-4 weeks (Canva is fast, Photoshop takes longer)

💰 KES 5,000-50,000 per design

How to start: Learn Canva (easier) or Photoshop → create portfolio → sell on Fiverr

Course at MTTI: Graphic Design (2 months, KES 12,000)

7. Virtual Assistant (Tech-Focused)

💼 Medium Demand Easy

What you'll do: Email management, scheduling, data entry for tech entrepreneurs.

Training needed: 1-2 weeks (mostly tools learning)

💰 KES 30,000-80,000/month

8. Mobile Phone Repair Technician

🔥 High Demand Medium

What you'll do: Screen replacement, battery swap, software fixes for phone owners.

Training needed: 4-8 weeks

💰 KES 50,000-150,000/month (very scalable)

What you'll do: Write simple Python scripts, automate tasks, small projects.

Training needed: 6-8 weeks (to "hireable" level)

💰 KES 50,000-200,000 per project (grows with experience)
