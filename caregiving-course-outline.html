CAREGIVING — CAG-01

Course Outline · TVET CDACC Level 4 aligned · "Start Learning, Start Earning"

11 units 292 lessons 590 curriculum hours 59 credits

CAREGIVING prepares learners to provide competent, professional care in homes, hospices and healthcare facilities — from housekeeping and dietary support through first aid, catering, and specialised care for geriatric, terminally ill, mentally ill, disabled, and special-needs clients. The course is built on the TVET CDACC Caregiving Level 4 curriculum, with a short orientation unit up front and three supplementary units layered in for practical, workplace-facing skills.

Entry requirements

  • Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE); or
  • National TVET Certificate in Home-Based Care Level 3, or a related trade area; or
  • Equivalent qualifications as determined by TVETA

An individual pursuing the full qualification is also required to complete a minimum of 480 hours of industrial (workplace) training in the health care sector, arranged separately from the units below.

Structure at a glance

#UnitTypeHoursCredits

Hours and credits shown for the 7 official units total 590 hours / 59 credits, matching the TVET CDACC Caregiving Level 4 pack exactly. The full qualification separately requires 480 hours of industrial (workplace) training, arranged outside this portal and not counted in the totals above. The 4 orientation/supplementary units add practical depth beyond the official pack and carry no separate credit weighting.

How assessment works Every topic ends in a scored quiz (50% pass mark). A unit is graded — and earns its own official transcript — once every quiz inside it is passed. The full course certificate is issued once all 11 units are passed and fees are fully cleared.
