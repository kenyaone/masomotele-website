Importance of Electrical Technology in Society

Why electricity matters

What is Electrical Technology?

Electrical Technology: The branch of technology that deals with the practical application of electricity — including generation, distribution, installation, and maintenance of electrical systems and equipment.
Electricity: A form of energy resulting from the movement of charged particles (electrons), used as a source of power for lighting, heating, motors, and electronics.

Safety Rules and Regulations

Your life depends on following safety rules

Why Safety Matters in Electrical Work

Electrical hazard: Any condition that exposes a person to risk of electric shock, electrocution, arc flash, arc blast, fire, or explosion from electrical equipment.
Safety regulation: A rule or standard designed to protect workers and the public from injury or death when working with electricity.

⚠️ Electricity is DEADLY — Always follow safety rules

Electricity can cause: electric shock, severe burns, cardiac arrest, fire, and death. Even low voltages (50V AC) can be fatal under certain conditions. Never take shortcuts with electrical safety.

Key Safety Rules in an Electrical Workplace

#RuleWhy It Matters
1Always isolate (switch off) before workingPrevents shock from live conductors
2Use correct PPE at all timesProtects body from shock, burns, falling objects
3Never work alone on live equipmentPartner can assist in emergencies
4Store tools properly after usePrevents accidents and tool damage
5Keep work area clean and dryWater conducts electricity — danger of shock
6Use insulated tools onlyInsulation prevents current flowing through you
7Label and lock out/tag out circuitsPrevents accidental energising
8Check equipment before useDamaged insulation = shock risk

Roles of Stakeholders in Electrical Safety

Who is responsible for keeping workers safe?

Key Stakeholders and Their Responsibilities

Stakeholder: Any person or organisation with an interest or responsibility in ensuring electrical safety in the workplace.
StakeholderRole in Electrical Safety
Workers / ElectriciansFollow all safety rules; use PPE; report hazards; attend safety training; care for tools and equipment
Employers / ContractorsProvide PPE and safe tools; conduct safety training; ensure safe working conditions; enforce regulations
Government (EPRA)Set and enforce electrical safety regulations; license electricians; inspect workplaces; prosecute violators
Kenya Power (KPLC)Maintain the national grid safely; respond to electrical emergencies; educate consumers
ERC / Energy RegulatorsRegulate energy sector; set standards for equipment; approve installations
Consumers / PublicUse electrical appliances safely; report faults; avoid illegal connections; engage certified electricians

📌 Key Regulatory Bodies in Kenya

• EPRA — Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority (regulates electrical installations)

• KPLC — Kenya Power and Lighting Company (supplies electricity)

• KEBS — Kenya Bureau of Standards (sets standards for electrical equipment)

• ERC — Energy Regulatory Commission (oversees energy sector policy)

⚠️ Illegal Connections

Connecting to the power supply without authorisation is illegal, dangerous, and punishable by law in Kenya. Always engage a licensed electrician and obtain permits from KPLC/EPRA before any installation work.

Proper Tool Storage

Tool care rule: Clean tools after use, store in a dry toolbox or panel, inspect insulation before each use, and replace damaged tools immediately. Never use a damaged insulated tool — replace it.

✏️ Tool Storage Best Practice

• Store tools in a clean, dry toolbox or wall-mounted panel.

• Keep cutting tools (knives, hacksaws) with blade guards or in sheaths.

• Label or colour-code insulated tools for easy identification.

• Inspect before use — check for cracked handles, damaged insulation, bent shafts.
