❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Can I really start a repair business after 2 months? ▼
Yes! 95% of our graduates start repair businesses within 3 months. You learn real skills on real hardware. The business module teaches pricing, customer management, and startup essentials. Many graduates operate from home initially.
What's the income potential? ▼
As a technician: KES 30,000-50,000/month. As a shop owner: KES 50,000-150,000+/month depending on volume. Our graduates average KES 30,000-80,000/month in their first year.
Is 2 months enough time to learn? ▼
Yes. Our intensive, hands-on approach teaches you 80% of what you need. The remaining 20% you learn on the job. We cover the most common repairs (70% of repair shop work). You'll be ready to handle routine repairs within weeks.
What if I don't want to start a business? ▼
Many graduates work as technicians at computer shops earning KES 30,000-50,000/month. High demand for skilled repair technicians. Job placement support included. Flexible career path.
What if I don't achieve business goals? ▼
We offer a 50% refund guarantee. If you complete the course but don't start a repair business or secure employment, we refund half your tuition. We're confident in our training.
How do the payment plans work? ▼
3 options: (1) KES 15,000 upfront, (2) KES 8,000 down + KES 7,000 at week 4, (3) Pay as you earn from repairs (contact us). You can start immediately after first payment.
