Programming Course Questions

What will I learn in programming?

Python fundamentals, variables, loops, functions, Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), data structures, algorithms, debugging, and real-world projects. You'll build actual applications and learn problem-solving skills that employers value.

Do I need math or prior coding experience?

No. Many students come with no programming background. We teach logic and problem-solving from scratch. You don't need advanced math—basic math is enough.

Will I really build projects?

Yes. You build real projects starting Week 2. By the end of the course, you'll have a portfolio of applications to show employers or clients. This is what makes you job-ready.

How long is the course?

2 months (8 weeks). Classes Monday-Saturday, 9 AM to 4 PM. Intense practical training with daily coding.

What's the fee?

KES 10,000 with flexible payment plans. You can pay weekly or monthly.

What jobs can I get?

Junior Developer (KES 80,000-150,000/month), Python developer, Web developer, Software tester, Freelance programmer. Many graduates also do freelance work earning KES 50,000-200,000+ per month.

Can I freelance after the course?

Yes, absolutely. Many graduates start freelancing on Upwork, Fiverr, or Toptal. Your portfolio of projects is your ticket to clients. Start earning within weeks of completing the course.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. TVETA-accredited certificate upon completion. Plus you get a portfolio of real projects—which is more valuable to employers than a certificate alone.

Is programming hard to learn?

Not as hard as many people think. It's about logic and practice, not genius. Our instructors make it click. If you can break problems into steps, you can code. Most students go from "I can't code" to "I just built an application" in 2 months.

What's the job market like for programmers?

Strong and growing. Every business needs software. Tech companies are hiring aggressively. Freelance rates are high. You'll have plenty of opportunities—both employment and freelance.
