Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to reach a conversational level? +

Most students reach conversational level (A2) within 2-3 months with consistent practice. By the end of our B1 level (12 weeks), you'll be able to handle professional conversations, job interviews, and everyday situations with confidence.

Do I need prior language learning experience? +

No! Our beginner level (A1) starts from scratch. Whether you've never studied a language or have previous experience, we have a level suited for you. We assess your current level before enrollment and place you in the right class.

What job opportunities exist for German speakers? +

Excellent opportunities exist in multiple sectors: Healthcare (hospitals in Germany/Middle East), Technology (software companies), Hospitality (tourism, hotels), Business Process Outsourcing (customer service), Translation/Interpretation, Education, and Export-Import companies. German speakers earn significantly more in most fields.

Can I work in Germany with just German language skills? +

German language skills are essential for working in Germany, but your professional qualifications matter too. German-speaking healthcare professionals are in high demand in Germany. We help prepare you with language skills, and we also offer vocational courses like CNA which are recognized in Germany.

Is there job placement help after the course? +

Yes! We provide comprehensive job placement support including CV writing in German, interview preparation, and connections with employers seeking German-speaking professionals. We have partnerships with companies in Kenya and internationally.

What's the difference between A1, A2, B1, and B2 levels? +

A1 (Beginner): Very basic communication, greetings, simple introductions
A2 (Elementary): Daily conversations, can handle routine situations
B1 (Intermediate): Professional conversations, work-ready, can discuss complex topics
B2 (Upper Intermediate): Fluent, can handle professional and academic settings

Can I combine German with other MTTI courses? +

Absolutely! Many students combine German with CNA, Cybersecurity, or other courses. You can take them simultaneously or sequentially. We offer a 15% bundle discount when you enroll in German plus another course. This combination significantly boosts your market value.
