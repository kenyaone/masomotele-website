❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Will I be able to work in Germany after German B1? ▼
Yes! B1 level is the standard for German job markets. With B1 certification, you can apply for nursing positions, tech jobs, tour guide roles, and more in Germany. MTTI provides visa and job placement support to help you transition.
How long does it take to reach B1 level? ▼
Typically 6-12 months for intensive study (3 levels: A1, A2, B1). Duration depends on your starting level and study commitment. We offer flexible daily and weekly class schedules to fit your pace.
Can I get German for FREE at MTTI? ▼
YES! If you enroll in ANY other MTTI course (CNA, Web Design, Programming, etc.), German language classes are included FREE. This is our signature offer - get 2 skills at the price of 1!
What salary can I expect in Germany? ▼
Healthcare: €2,500-3,500/month. IT/Tech: €2,800-4,500/month. Hospitality: €2,000-2,800/month. Plus benefits. Minimum wage in Germany is €12/hour. Many MTTI graduates work in Germany earning 5-10x what they earn in Kenya.
What if I don't reach B1 level? ▼
We offer a 50% refund guarantee if you complete the course but don't achieve B1 level. We're confident 95% of our students reach B1 within the timeframe.
How do payment plans work? ▼
3 options: (1) KES 24,000 per level upfront, (2) KES 6,000/month × 4 months, (3) FREE if you enroll in another MTTI course. You can start immediately after first payment.
