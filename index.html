FAQ

Common Questions

How long are your courses?

Our courses range from 2 weeks (Computer Essentials) to 8 weeks for most technical courses like Computer Applications, Graphic Design, Web Design, Cybersecurity, Computer Repair, Networking, and Mobile Phone Repair. AI is 4 weeks, while Healthcare Support Assistant (CNA) is 6 months and German Language runs 6–8 months per level. We have a schedule that fits everyone.

Do you offer certificates?

Yes! All our courses come with TVETA-accredited certificates upon completion. These are government-recognized and trusted by employers across Kenya.

What are the class schedules?

We offer morning, afternoon, and weekend classes to accommodate different lifestyles. Contact us and we'll find a schedule that works for you.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans. You can pay in installments throughout the course duration. Talk to our admissions team for your options.

Where are you located?

We're located at Sagaas Centre, 4th Floor, Eldoret, Kenya. Easily accessible by matatu or personal vehicle. Contact us for directions.

What are the course fees?

Our fees vary by course, from as low as KES 1,500 for short courses to KES 59,000 for comprehensive programs like CNA. Download our complete fees structure here for detailed pricing on each course. All courses include TVETA accreditation and flexible payment plans are available.
