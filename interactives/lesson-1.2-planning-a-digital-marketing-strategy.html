1. Why plan at all?

Sarah's cousin says "just start posting on Facebook and see what happens." That is the #1 mistake small businesses make.

A strategy answers three questions BEFORE spending a shilling:
  1. What am I actually trying to achieve?
  2. Who am I trying to reach?
  3. How will I know it's working?

7. Access, security & consistency

  • Least-privilege access — only give admin rights to those who need them
  • Approval workflow — every post reviewed before publish
  • Response protocol — how to reply to complaints, DMs, reviews
  • Crisis plan — bad review, data breach, viral complaint
  • Passwords — long, unique, in a manager, 2FA enabled
  • Brand consistency — same logo, colours, tone across every channel
Get this wrong on day 1, and by month 3 someone else is running your Instagram.
