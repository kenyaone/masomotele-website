2. The 3 numbers that decide everything

Total spend: KES 1,000/day × 7 days = KES 7,000 max
Gross profit per sale: KES 1,500 × 40% = KES 600
Max profitable CPA: KES 600 — above this, Mwema loses money on every sale.
Target conversions to hit ROAS 3×:
Spend × ROAS ÷ Order value = 7,000 × 3 ÷ 1,500 = ≥ 14 sales just to break the ROAS bar
Client target = 30+ conversions AND ROAS ≥ 3× both must pass.

3. Why clicks are not enough

The mindshift: clicks are an intermediate metric. Conversions are the outcome metric.

Consider this: if a click costs KES 50 and Mwema gets one sale per 100 clicks, that is KES 5,000 spent to earn KES 1,500 in revenue — ROAS 0.3×. Losing money.

Every trainee walks into this lab focused on cost per click. Every trainee who passes walks out focused on cost per acquisition (CPA). That mental shift is the whole point of Module 10.

7. Self-check quiz

Ten questions. Answer AFTER you've hit the green pill in the simulator.

1. Mwema's max profitable CPA is…

Correct — 1,500 × 40% margin = 600. Above KES 600, Mwema loses money on every sale.

2. Clicks vs conversions — which is the outcome metric?

Correct — clicks are intermediate. Conversions are the outcome the business actually cares about.

3. If ROAS is 0.3×, the campaign is…

Correct — ROAS 0.3× means every KES 1 of ad spend returns only KES 0.30 of revenue.

4. Broad, Phrase, Exact — which usually converts best?

Correct — Exact match brings the fewest but highest-intent searchers.

5. Negative keywords help by…

Correct — negatives remove wasted clicks and improve efficiency.

6. Landing page quality directly affects…

Correct — a fast, relevant landing page converts more visitors into sales.

7. Mwema's targets — both must pass. What are they?

Correct — the client pays only if BOTH conditions hit.

8. The sweet spot for max CPC bid in this lab?

Correct — this is the profitable zone for Mwema's numbers.

9. Ad copy quality of 3/10 will…

Correct — bad copy costs more per click AND fewer people click it. Both directions hurt.

10. You have hit ROAS 4× but only 12 conversions. What do you do?

Correct — the campaign is profitable per sale but not producing enough volume. Push slightly on budget/bid.
