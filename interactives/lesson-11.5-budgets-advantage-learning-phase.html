2. Advantage+ Campaign Budget — the toggle

Advantage+ (previously CBO) lets Meta re-distribute your campaign-level budget in real time across ad sets — more to the winner, less to the laggard. Sarah moves to it the day she launches her third ad set.

Rule: 1–2 ad sets → ad-set-level budgets are fine. 3+ ad sets → Advantage+ Campaign Budget almost always wins.

4. Learning Phase status pill

Every new ad set enters a Learning Phase — Meta's algorithm needs ~50 optimisation events in a rolling 7-day window to stabilise. Below 50 → wild swings. Above 50 → predictable cost per result.

● In learning Advantage+ Sales — 32/50 optimisation events (7d)
32/50

Do NOT edit the ad set. Any budget, audience, or creative change resets the 50-event counter.

3 events that RESET learning: changing budget by more than 20%, changing audience or placements, replacing the creative. Duplicate the ad set for the test — don't touch the live one.

6. Kill-decision challenge

Three triggers for killing an ad set: frequency > 3, CPA > target × 5, ROAS drop > 30% held 4 days. Score out of 4.
