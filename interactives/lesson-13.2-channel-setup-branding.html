1. Personal vs Brand account

Every YouTube channel sits under a Google account. Two flavours — pick the second.

4. The About tab — SEO-load-bearing text

The About tab tells YouTube's algorithm what the channel is about, and gives new viewers the trust signals to subscribe. Four elements — every one non-optional.

  1. Channel description. 200–400 words. Keywords front-loaded in the first sentence.
  2. Business email. Enables brand-deal inbounds and press. Skipping this signals "hobbyist".
  3. Country: Kenya. Powers the "recommended for you" localisation.
  4. Links. Website, WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok. Every relevant social. Max 14.
Sarah's first sentence: "Karibu Kitchen is Eldoret's home-cooked lunch delivery service." One sentence = business + city + category. YouTube's classifier reads this before anything else.
