1. The rule beginners break

Sarah signs in at ads.google.com, not YouTube. Same Google account she uses for Search ads. Same billing. Different campaign type — Video.

Most common beginner mistake: Trying to boost a video from inside YouTube Studio. There is no "Boost Post" button. Every paid impression runs through Google Ads.

3. Kenya locked. Ages 18–55. Why?

Sarah's delivery zone is Eldoret — Kapsoya, Elgon View, CBD. Locking Kenya + a bid boost on the Eldoret post-code is standard practice. Ages 18–55 covers university students through mid-career office workers — her actual paying customer base.

Custom intent keywords beat every other targeting for local businesses. They tell Google: "Show my ad only to people who recently searched for these exact phrases on Google or YouTube." Zero wasted impressions on Nairobi office workers who cannot receive her delivery.

4. Budget input in KES

Change the budget input above to see how the forecast moves. Notes:

  • KES 5,000/month → about 420 paid views/week. Enough to test creative, not enough to grow.
  • KES 15,000/month (Sarah's) → about 1,250 paid views/week. Break-even at ~2% delivery conversion.
  • KES 50,000/month → about 4,200 paid views/week. Requires 3+ ad variants to prevent creative fatigue.
Kenyan startup rule: KES 10,000–20,000/month is the sweet spot for a single-location business. Below KES 5,000 you cannot outbid competitors. Above KES 50,000 you outrun your creative supply.

Never do: Bumper ads for conversions. Video action for pure reach. The format determines what Google's algorithm optimises for — mismatch it and you burn budget.
