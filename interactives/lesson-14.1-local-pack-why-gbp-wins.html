1. What Google Business Profile is

Google Business Profile (GBP) — call it G-B-P — is the free listing that shows your business inside Google Search and Google Maps. It was called Google My Business until 2021 when Google renamed it and moved management into the Business Profile Manager.

Sarah Chebet's Karibu Kitchen listing appears with a photo, opening hours, phone number, directions button and reviews on the right-hand side of results — and inside the map results at the top.

Completely free. No ads. No monthly fee. Just a properly filled-in profile.

2. The three surfaces a verified GBP listing appears on

Surface 1 — Local Pack

The top three business listings with a small map, shown for any search Google thinks is local.

Surface 2 — Google Maps

Both the mobile app and the desktop version. Fully searchable and filterable.

Surface 3 — Knowledge Panel

The side card on Search when a searcher looks up your brand name directly.

One profile. Three surfaces. Zero cost. All three unlock the instant verification completes.

4. The three ranking factors

Google decides who sits at position 1 of the pack using three factors. Together, and only together.

Factor 1 — Relevance

Does the listing MATCH the query? Google reads three fields: primary category (Meal delivery service, not Restaurant), additional categories (up to nine), and the business description (750 characters). Services and menu items also feed relevance.

Factor 2 — Distance

Google measures the physical distance between the searcher's location and your business address. The one factor you cannot control. Two responses: set a Service Area listing every estate you deliver to (Sarah lists Eldoret CBD, Kapsoya, Elgon View, Racecourse, Pioneer), and make relevance + prominence strong enough that Google shows you across a wider radius.

Factor 3 — Prominence

How well-known Google thinks your business is. Built from four things: review quantity + quality, review recency, links and mentions across the web, and Google's own confidence signals (age, NAP consistency).

The compounding trick: Relevance is fast (edit fields today). Distance is fixed. Prominence takes months but keeps stacking. Move all three and you climb.
