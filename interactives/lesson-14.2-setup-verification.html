1. Sign in at business.google.com

Open business.google.com. Sign in with the Gmail address Sarah will use to manage the profile forever — usually a business Gmail like sarah@karibukitchen.co.ke, not her personal one.

NAP rule: Name, Address and Phone on the profile must match the storefront, the website and Facebook exactly. One missing full stop breaks Google's trust match.
