2. The 750-character business description — keyword logic

Google gives you 750 characters, but only the first 250 show without a "more" click. Every high-value keyword lives in the first 250 chars. Keywords watched:

  • lunch delivery — the target discovery query
  • Eldoret — the town
  • Kapsoya — the neighbourhood

Edit the textarea in the mock above. The green panel shows a live keyword scan.

Natural writing beats stuffing. Google's algorithm penalises listings that repeat "lunch delivery Eldoret" seven times. Write the way a real customer would explain the business to a friend.

3. Attributes and hours — the small ticks that move filters

Attributes decide which filters your listing appears under. If a searcher taps "delivery" in the Google Maps filter panel and you haven't ticked Delivery, you are invisible for that whole search — even if you deliver.

Regular hours + special hours is the difference between "the food was hot" and "I drove there and they were closed for Madaraka Day". Special hours prevent the #1 worst customer experience.
