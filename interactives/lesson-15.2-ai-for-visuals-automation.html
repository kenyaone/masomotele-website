$10+/mo

Free tier · Creative Cloud

Trained on commercially safe data. Best for brand/agency work.

6. Ethics + credits + Kenyan context

  1. Never fake customer testimonials — AI can generate them but it's fraud + illegal under Kenyan consumer law.
  2. Disclose AI images for high-stakes claims (medical, financial). Not always legally required — always ethical.
  3. Copyright — AI-generated images CANNOT be copyrighted in the US. Kenya law is unclear. Assume you own the output for practical purposes.
  4. Don't reproduce known people — do not generate images of specific real Kenyan celebrities, politicians, or your neighbours without permission.
  5. Watermarks — some tools add invisible watermarks. Fine for marketing use but disclosed for editorial.
Kenyan-specific: AI can generate "authentic Kenyan cuisine" imagery — but often gets it wrong (wrong utensils, wrong plating, non-Kenyan produce). Always sanity-check before publishing.
