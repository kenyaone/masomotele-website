1. Sarah's problem

Sarah's Facebook page has 340 friends. Her cousin says a website is "unnecessary — everyone is on Facebook and WhatsApp." Meanwhile Nyakinyua Kitchen — her direct competitor two blocks away — has a plain WordPress site that ranks #1 for "lunch delivery Eldoret". Sarah has never appeared. She has KES 8,000 for a website and no idea where to spend it.

4. The 4 steps to build a website

Step 1 — Register domain (.co.ke)KES 1,000–3,000 / year
Step 2 — Register hostingKES 2,000–8,000 / year
Step 3 — Design + buildDIY: free · Freelancer: KES 15k–100k+
Step 4 — Promote (SEO, social, ads)Ongoing
Skip Step 4 and nobody will ever visit the site. Building without promoting is like renting a shop in a desert.
