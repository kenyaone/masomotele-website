1. What is SEO?

Search Engine Optimisation = improving your website's visibility in the unpaid results of search engines. When Nyakinyua Kitchen appears first for "lunch delivery Eldoret", that's SEO. When Sarah doesn't appear at all, that's no SEO.

The hard truth: SEO takes 3–6 months of consistent work before real results. Start today, not next month.

4. Long-tail beats short-tail

Short-tail
Impossible for SME
Mid-tail
Slow to rank
Long-tail (4+ words, specific, local)
Rankable in weeks

Big keywords like "food delivery" are impossible for a small business. Long-tail (4+ words) is the answer.

Rule: one long-tail keyword at #1 > five short-tail keywords at #40.

5. The 6 on-page elements

1. Page title

50–60 chars · keyword first

2. URL

Clean · lowercase · hyphens · keyword

3. Meta description

120–160 chars · keyword · CTA

4. Heading tags

ONE H1 per page · H2/H3 for sub

5. Alt text

Every image · screen readers + SEO

6. 300+ words body

Relevant, structured, keyword 3–5×
