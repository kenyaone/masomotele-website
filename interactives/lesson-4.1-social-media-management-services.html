4. Batch vs realtime

✗ Amateur — realtime posting

  • Open 3 apps × 3 times/week
  • 90 min/week of app-switching
  • Skip a day → skip 2 → rhythm gone
  • Nyakinyua Kitchen's approach

✓ Professional — batch scheduling

  • 1 hour on Monday morning
  • Write whole week's content once
  • Schedule + close the app
  • Posts publish while she's at a wedding
