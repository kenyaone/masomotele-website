1. Sarah's budget conversation

Sarah's cousin's colleague quoted KES 40,000/month for "a complete package". Sarah can afford KES 15,000. She needs to know where to spend it for maximum impact.

By the end of Module 5, Sarah will know exactly which platform, which objective, and which ad format fits her KES 15,000.

2. The 2 big families

🔍 Search Engine Marketing (SEM)

Ads that appear when someone TYPES a query. High buyer intent.

Google Ads (90%+ share) Microsoft Ads (Bing) Yahoo Ads

📱 Social + Display

Ads that appear in feeds, stories, videos, or on other sites. Discovery.

Meta Ads (FB+IG) LinkedIn TikTok YouTube X / Twitter GDN (display)

3. The 7 ad formats

1. Search ads

Text ads at top of Google results

2. Display / banner

Image ads on websites/apps

3. Video ads

Pre/mid/post-roll video

4. Image ads

Static/animated in feeds

5. Pop-up ads

Separate window overlays

6. Floating ads

Sticky overlay on page

7. Sponsored posts

Paid content that looks native

4. The 4 questions to choose a platform

Answer these four questions in order. The right platform reveals itself.

1
Who is my customer?
Sarah: Kenyan office worker or family in Eldoret
2
Where does that customer spend time online?
Sarah: Facebook + Instagram (mostly). Some Google.
3
What am I selling?
Sarah: Delivery order — transactional purchase
4
How urgent is the decision?
Sarah: Very — hungry office workers decide in an hour

6. Self-check quiz

1. The 2 big families of online advertising?

Correct — Search Engine Marketing vs Social/Display.

2. Which platform runs Facebook AND Instagram AND Messenger from one dashboard?

Correct — one platform for all Meta properties.

3. Google Ads share of the SEM market?

Correct — dominant globally.

4. What's a "testing reserve" budget?

Correct — every professional marketer keeps one.

5. Sarah should spend most of her budget on…

Correct — her customers spend time on FB+IG.

6. Which is NOT one of the 7 ad formats?

Correct — SMS is direct marketing, not an ad format.

7. Why Google Search Ads for Sarah?

Correct — someone typing "lunch delivery Eldoret" is at the moment of decision.

8. Sarah's LinkedIn budget?

Correct — wrong platform for her customer.

9. What's Q1 of the 4-question choice framework?

Correct — everything else follows.

10. YouTube Ads are managed inside…

Correct — YouTube ads are a Google Ads campaign type.
