1. The 87% mobile reality

87% of Sarah's traffic is mobile

Not 30%. Not 50%. Eighty-seven per cent. This is not a marketing statistic — it's a strategic instruction. Every website, every ad, every email must be designed for the phone first. Desktop comes second.

2. 5 mobile advantages

  1. Reach — near-universal mobile use in Kenya
  2. Location-based targeting — down to 1 km precision
  3. Immediate reach — users check phones dozens of times/day
  4. Higher engagement — mobile beats desktop on interaction rates
  5. Personalisation — every phone is ONE person, not a shared PC

5. Location targeting — Sarah's 3 pins

Mobile's killer feature: geo-fencing. Show ads ONLY to phones physically inside a chosen radius. Sarah's Facebook campaign targets three pins:

📍
Kapsoya (5 km)
📍
Elgon View (5 km)
📍
Racecourse (5 km)
Under-used by Kenyan SMEs. Most SMEs target "Kenya" or "Nairobi" — 99% of budget wasted on people who can't reach them. Pins turn this on its head.

7. Sarah's mobile-first content plan

Karibu Kitchen mobile-first checklist

  • Every food photo shot portrait (9:16), not landscape
  • Every video vertical 9:16, ≤ 30 seconds
  • Every headline ≤ 60 characters
  • Every button ≥ 44 px tall (thumb-tappable)
  • Every form ≤ 3 fields
  • Every menu inside a ☰ hamburger at the top
  • Every image WebP, under 100 KB (Lesson 9.4)
