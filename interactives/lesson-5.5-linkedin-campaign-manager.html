1. Meet Wangari — B2B wellness consultancy

Sarah is B2C. LinkedIn is not her platform. Wangari is a different persona for a different platform.

W

Wangari — Wangari's Wellness, Nairobi

Corporate mental-health workshops for insurance and banking firms. Target buyer: HR Director / Head of People at 200+ staff financial services companies. Average deal: KES 350,000/workshop. First LinkedIn budget: USD 800 (≈ KES 100,000).

Why LinkedIn only makes sense for Wangari: a single closed workshop pays back her entire ad budget 3.5×. Sarah's KES 350 lunches could never justify USD 10 clicks. LinkedIn only works when the deal size covers the click price.

2. The 4-level hierarchy

Meta had three levels. LinkedIn has four — the extra level (Campaign Group) is for reporting rollup.

LEVEL 1

Account

Billing, users, and the LinkedIn Insight Tag (LinkedIn's Pixel equivalent).

▼
LEVEL 2

Campaign Group (optional but useful)

Portfolio of related campaigns. Wangari's: "Q1 HR Directors" and "Q2 Compliance Officers".

▼
LEVEL 3

Campaign

Objective, audience, placement, budget, schedule, bidding.

▼
LEVEL 4

Ad

The creative — image, headline, body, CTA.
