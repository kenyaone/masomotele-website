4. The 4 questions — reapplied

Answer for any client. Same 4 questions. Different platform answers.

1
Who is my customer? Age, role, location.
2
Where do they spend time online? Facebook? LinkedIn? TikTok?
3
What am I selling? Deal size, purchase type.
4
How urgent is the decision? Now, this week, this quarter?

5. 5 common mistakes

1. All budget on 1 platform in month 1

Test at least 2.

2. Judging before Learning Phase exits

50 events, not 50 days.

3. Wrong creative for platform

Studio ads on TikTok. Selfies on LinkedIn. Kills every campaign.

4. No Pixel / Events API

You're flying blind.

5. Chasing the trending platform

Match customer first. Platform second. Trends never.
