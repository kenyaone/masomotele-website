1. What GA4 measures

  • WHO visits — unique users, location, device, language
  • WHAT they do — pages, time on site, clicks, scrolls
  • WHETHER they convert — buy, submit form, subscribe
Free forever for standard use. Every Kenyan business site should install it.

2. The 8 essential terms

Unique visitors

Individual people (not repeat visits)

Impressions

Times content was displayed

Clicks

Times users clicked a link/ad

Bounce rate

% leaving after 1 page only

Conversion rate

% who completed desired action

CTR

Clicks ÷ Impressions

Tracking code

The JS snippet that collects data

Referrals

Traffic from other sites linking to you

5. Sarah's month-3 review — the trap

What Sarah sees on the surface

  • Ad spend: KES 45,000
  • Purchases: 58
  • Revenue: KES 49,300

"Spent 45k, made 49k — that's basically a loss. Kill the ads."

The truth — 3 numbers she missed

  • Profit ≠ Revenue. Profit per order (after cost) = KES 350. 58 × 350 = KES 20,300 profit.
  • View-through conversions. Meta reports 12 additional purchases attributed to view-only ad exposure — not in her 58.
  • Lifetime value. 19 of those 58 customers have re-ordered organically since. Their real value is 3–5× first purchase.

Sarah's ads ARE profitable. She just didn't see the full picture. This is why analytics matters — surface numbers lie.
