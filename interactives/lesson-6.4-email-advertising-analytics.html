5. Google Quality Score slider

The hidden number every Google Ads professional lives by. Drag the slider to see the effect.

Google Quality Score (1–10)

7
3 ways to raise Quality Score:
  1. Rewrite ad copy to include the EXACT keyword you're bidding on
  2. Improve landing page (ad about delivery → land on Delivery page, not Home)
  3. Pause underperforming keywords — one bad one drags down the ad group

6. Professional monthly report — 5 sections

  1. Goal this month
  2. What was actually achieved — numbers, not adjectives
  3. What worked — winning ad/platform/content
  4. What did NOT work — and why
  5. What changes are planned next month
Include actual CPA, ROAS, QS changes, and 2-week trend. Miss any of these 5 and the client loses trust. Include them every month and they keep paying you.
