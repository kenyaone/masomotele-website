2. Scenario challenge — pick the best format

Three messages Sarah needs to publish today. For each, pick the format most likely to land the message.

Scenario 1 — Announce a same-day promo "Today only: KES 350 chapati-mayai combo with free delivery in Kapsoya between 12–2 PM." Sarah needs high urgency and instant clarity.
Correct: Image with price overlay. Promo needs one glance. Add "TODAY ONLY · KES 350 · WhatsApp 0712-XXX-XXX" burned into the image so it survives being screenshotted and shared to WhatsApp Status.
Scenario 2 — Tell a customer story Joyce Wanjiru's office ordered lunch for 12 people during a rough project week. She sent Sarah a tearful voice note thanking the team.
Correct: Short vertical video. Stories carry emotion. A 30-second video of Joyce's team eating + captions with her quote earns saves and shares — carousels tell facts, video tells feeling.
Scenario 3 — Teach a recipe Sarah wants to publish her signature 6-step ugali-and-sukuma recipe as free content, showing expertise and getting saves.
Correct: 6-slide carousel. Instructional content wins on carousels — one step per slide gives readers control, drives Save actions (weighted heavily by Instagram's algorithm), and lets them return without re-watching a video.

4. The 90-second content check

Before you press Post, run every visual through these 5 questions. Blurry photos are worse than no post.

  1. Is it sharp? Zoom to 100%. If the food edges are fuzzy, re-shoot.
  2. Is it well-lit? Natural light beats artificial light every time.
  3. Real person / place / product? Not a stock model, not a generic photo.
  4. Does it match your brand? Same colours and style as last week's post.
  5. Message in 3 seconds? A viewer scrolling should get it without reading the caption.
Ninety seconds. Five questions. Every visual. Every time. Sarah runs this check on her Sunday-afternoon shoots — the plates that fail get eaten, not posted.

The five research numbers that make the case

  • 2.3× — posts with images get roughly 2.3× the engagement of text-only posts (2024 HubSpot cross-platform study).
  • 1.8× — video content increases conversion rates by around 80% versus static media on landing pages.
  • 3× — infographics are shared roughly 3× more often than any other content type on Facebook and LinkedIn.
  • 65% — customers who see branded visuals recall the brand a week later, vs 10% for text-only.
  • ~85% — mobile users scroll past text-only posts within 1.5 seconds, whether they intended to or not.
What to stop doing: three habits that quietly kill Kenyan small-business content. Stop stock imagery — customers spot "smiling Kenyan office worker" in a second. Stop heavy filters — 2018 Instagram aesthetics look dated in 2026. Stop text-only posts — if you don't have time for a visual, don't post.
