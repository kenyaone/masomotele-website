5. Rule of thirds — try it

Imagine the image divided into a 3×3 grid. Place your subject on one of the four intersection points. Click each position and see the difference.

🍲
Dead centre works but feels static. Try any of the four intersection points for a stronger, more balanced image.
On your phone: Every phone camera has a "grid" option in the settings. Turn it on. Frame your subject on an intersection point.

6. Brand consistency — the compound rule

Boring in the short term. Powerful in the long term. Every consistent design compounds into brand recognition.

ElementSarah's ruleWhy
ColoursMaroon + gold. Always.Instant recognition at 30 metres.
LogoSame file. Same size. Same corner.Muscle memory for viewers.
FontsOne heading font, one body font.Anything else looks amateur.
Photo treatmentSame filter every time.Feed looks like one brand, not 20 posts.
Tone of voiceWarm + friendly. Never formal.Personality customers can recognise.
Anti-pattern: "Nyakinyua Kitchen" changes their colours every week. Nobody can identify their brand at a glance. Consistency loses in a day and wins in a year.

8. Self-check quiz

Ten questions.

1. Which colour signals TRUST for a Kenyan bank?

Correct — blue is used by Equity, KCB, and virtually every bank worldwide for a reason.

2. What is Rule 1 of typography?

Correct — more than 3 fonts looks amateur. One for headlines, one for body.

3. What is visual hierarchy?

Correct — hierarchy = intended order of viewing. Photo first, price second, CTA third.

4. What percentage of a social graphic should be empty (white space)?

Correct — 20% minimum. Not literally white — just free of elements.

5. Rule of thirds — where do you place the subject?

Correct — the four intersection points create stronger, more balanced compositions than dead centre.

6. Sarah's brand colours are maroon + gold. Should she use blue on Friday's post?

Correct — consistency compounds. Random colour changes destroy brand recognition.

7. What are the three questions in the 5-second brand test?

Correct — if any of these three fails, the design fails.

8. For a food photo on Instagram, what should take ~70% of the space?

Correct — the food is the star. Everything else supports.

9. Which colour is associated with URGENCY and used in flash sales?

Correct — red = energy, urgency, passion. Coca-Cola, KFC, flash sales.

10. Which is a sign of an amateur design?

Correct — cluttered = amateur. Professional designs discipline themselves to leave space.
