1. What plugins do + the warning

Plugins are add-ons that extend WordPress. Contact forms. Booking. Analytics. Live chat. 60,000+ free in the directory.

Critical warning: every plugin you install:
  • Slows your site down slightly
  • Adds a possible security vulnerability
Rules: install only what you use · delete unused · update monthly · research before adding.
