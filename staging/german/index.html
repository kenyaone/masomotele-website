Common Questions
Frequently Asked Questions
Can I pay in installments?▼

Yes! You pay per module — KES 24,000 at a time. No need to pay the full KES 96,000 upfront. Start A1, and when you're ready for A2, pay then. We also accept M-Pesa payments.

Do I need IELTS or any English test?▼

No! Germany requires German language proficiency (B1/B2), not English. You'll be learning and working in German. No IELTS, TOEFL, or PTE needed.

What if I miss a class?▼

We understand you're working professionals. Missed class notes and catch-up materials are provided. Weekend revision sessions are also available to help you stay on track.

Will you help me find a job in Germany?▼

Yes! We connect our B1/B2 graduates with recruitment agencies that specialize in placing Kenyan healthcare workers in Germany. Many agencies handle visa processing, credential recognition, and relocation — often at no cost to you since the German employer pays.

Is German really that hard to learn?▼

Honestly? No! Many German words are similar to English (Telefon, Computer, Hospital/Krankenhaus). With consistent evening classes, most students hold basic conversations within 2 weeks. Our tutors make it fun and practical with healthcare-focused vocabulary from day one.

I'm a student nurse — should I start now or wait until I graduate?▼

Start NOW! If you begin German during your studies, you can be B2-ready by graduation. That means you can apply for Germany positions immediately instead of waiting another 8-10 months. Your classmates will still be job-hunting in Kenya while you're flying to Munich.

Can caregivers (not just nurses) work in Germany?▼

Absolutely! Altenpflege (elderly care) is actually Germany's BIGGEST demand area. Caregivers and care assistants are urgently needed. The requirements are similar — German B1/B2 level. Some employers accept B1 for caregiver roles. We also offer CNA (Certified Nursing Assistant) courses alongside German.

I'm a Form 4 leaver — can I go to Germany without a degree?▼

Yes! Germany's Ausbildung (paid apprenticeship) system is designed for high school graduates. You need your KCSE certificate (D+ minimum, C- for healthcare) and German B1/B2 proficiency. You'll train for 2–3 years in a real company — earning €800–€1,300/month while studying — and graduate into a full-time career paying €2,000–€3,600/month. Careers include nursing, electrician, mechanic, hotel management, kindergarten teaching, and solar energy. Start learning German at MTTI now and be ready to apply!

What is Ausbildung and how does it work?▼

Ausbildung is Germany's world-famous dual vocational training system. You work part-time in a company (hospital, workshop, hotel, etc.) while attending vocational school. Training lasts 2–3 years. You earn a monthly salary throughout. Tuition is completely free — the government and employer cover everything. At the end, you receive a German-recognized diploma and can work anywhere in Germany or the EU. Over 70,000 Ausbildung positions are unfilled in Germany right now — the demand is enormous.

What's included in the KES 24,000 per module?▼

Everything! Tuition, learning materials, medical German vocabulary module, exam preparation, and even FREE Computer Essentials training covering Computer Hardware & Basic Components, File Management, Operating Systems (Windows, macOS), and Keyboard & Mouse Skills. The only additional cost is the Goethe/telc exam fee when you're ready to certify (paid separately to the exam body).
