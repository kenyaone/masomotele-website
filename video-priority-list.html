Video Production Priority List

Every course's "Video" lesson slot is currently an empty placeholder (students see a friendly "Video Coming Soon" message and can continue regardless). This list ranks which topics genuinely benefit most from real filmed demonstration, focused on the four hands-on courses: CAG-01 (Caregiving), HCS-01 (Health Care Support/CNA), MOR-01 (Mobile Repair), CCI-01 (CCTV Installation).

Tier 1 — Film First Highest value

Physical technique that's genuinely hard to teach without watching it performed — hand positioning, pacing, and safety cues that text and quizzes can't fully convey.

CAG-01 — Caregiving

  • Unit: First Aid ServicesChoking ResponseShow full back-blow/abdominal-thrust sequence on a mannequin.
  • Unit: First Aid ServicesCPR Basics and the Recovery PositionHand placement, compression rate/depth, recovery position rollover.
  • Unit: First Aid ServicesWound Care and Bleeding ControlDressing application and direct-pressure technique.
  • Unit: First Aid ServicesBurns and Poisoning: Immediate Response
  • Unit: Special Needs Care SupportSafe Lifting, Transfers and Mobility AssistanceCorrect body mechanics — this is an injury-prevention skill for the caregiver too.
  • Unit: Special Needs Care SupportFeeding and Personal Care Techniques
  • Unit: House Keeping ServicesBed MakingHospital-corner technique, changing linen with a client still in bed.

HCS-01 — Health Care Support / CNA

  • Unit: First Aid ServicesResponding to a Choking Emergency (Virtual Lab)
  • Unit: Infection Prevention And ControlChoosing the Right PPE (Virtual Lab)Full donning/doffing sequence — order matters and is easy to get wrong from text alone.
  • Unit: Patient Care SupportSafe Patient Positioning and Fall Prevention (Virtual Lab)
  • Unit: Patient Care SupportAssisting with Activities of Daily Living
  • Unit: Special Needs Care SupportAssisting Clients with Mobility and Daily Living Challenges

MOR-01 — Mobile Repair

  • Unit: Screen and Battery ReplacementScreen Replacement ProcedureExact tool use and prying technique — the highest-risk-of-damage step in the whole course.
  • Unit: Screen and Battery ReplacementSafe Battery Replacement and DisposalSwelling/puncture risk — safety framing matters as much as technique.
  • Unit: Charging Port and Component-Level RepairCharging Port Replacement
  • Unit: Water Damage Diagnostics and Component TestingCleaning and Drying Procedures

CCI-01 — CCTV Installation

  • Unit: Installation Techniques and ConfigurationMounting Cameras Safely and Correctly
  • Unit: Installation Techniques and ConfigurationRunning and Terminating CablesConnector crimping/termination is the classic video-taught cabling skill.

Tier 2 — Worth Doing Eventually

Soft-skill demonstration or moderate-value technique — helpful, but the existing text/practical content already covers the material reasonably well.

  • CAG-01 / HCS-01Psychosocial Support topics — Active Listening and Empathy, Dementia Support, Grief/Loss and End-of-Life SupportRole-play style demos would help but aren't essential.
  • CAG-01Caregiving Catering Services — Cooking Techniques for Client Meals
  • HCS-01Human Anatomy And Physiology — Vital Signs and What They Tell You (Config Lab)Taking BP/pulse/temperature is a demonstrable technique.
  • CCI-01CCTV Components — Cabling Standards and ConnectorsShowing connector types by hand.
  • MOR-01Mobile Device Fundamentals and Tools — ESD Safety and Handling Sensitive Components

Tier 3 — Skip / Not Worth Filming

Conceptual, documentation-heavy, or already better served by the interactive practice tool than a passive video would be.

  • All "AI Tools for..." units (CAG-01, MOR-01, CCI-01) — conceptual, already covered by DiagnosticSim practicals
  • HCS-01's 13 computer-literacy/Office units (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Google Docs, Windows, Keyboard/Mouse, File Management, etc.) — the built simulators are the practice mechanism; the student doing it themselves beats watching someone else click through it
  • Pre-Departure Process (CAG-01 & HCS-01) — documentation/orientation content, not a physical technique
  • Medical Terminology, Hospital Store Procedures, Medical Legal Ethics — pure knowledge content
  • Work Ethics and Practices, Entrepreneurial Skills — soft knowledge content
