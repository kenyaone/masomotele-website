# SEO Recommender - Quick Start Guide

## 5-Minute Setup

### 1. Install & Activate (2 minutes)
- Upload `seo-recommender-plugin` folder to `/wp-content/plugins/`
- Go to Plugins → Activate "SEO Recommender"
- See "SEO Recommender" in sidebar ✓

### 2. Analyze Your First Post (2 minutes)
- Click: **SEO Recommender → Analyze Content**
- Select a post from the dropdown
- Click: **Analyze**
- Read recommendations

### 3. Make Improvements (1 minute)
- Click the recommendation issue
- Follow the suggestion
- Resave your post
- See score improve!

## Understanding Your SEO Score

```
90-100%  → Excellent (keep it up!)
70-89%   → Good (a few tweaks needed)
50-69%   → Fair (address warnings)
<50%     → Needs work (address critical issues)
```

## Common Recommendations & Fixes

### ❌ "Missing Meta Description"
**What:** No description in search results
**Fix:** Add 120-160 character summary of your post
**Example:** "Learn how to optimize your WordPress site for SEO with these 5 proven strategies."

### ❌ "Missing H1 Tag"
**What:** No main heading
**Fix:** Add an `<h1>` heading at top of content
**Example:** `<h1>How to Improve Your SEO Score</h1>`

### ⚠️ "Content Too Short"
**What:** Less than 300 words
**Fix:** Add more detailed information
**Tip:** Aim for 800+ words for best results

### ⚠️ "No Internal Links"
**What:** No links to other pages
**Fix:** Link to 2-3 related posts
**Example:** "[Read our guide to keywords](link-to-post)"

### ⚠️ "No Images"
**What:** Text-only content
**Fix:** Add 1-2 relevant images
**Bonus:** Improves visual appeal and engagement

### ℹ️ "Short Title"
**What:** Title less than 30 characters
**Fix:** Make title more descriptive
**Before:** "SEO Tips"
**After:** "10 Best SEO Tips for 2024"

## Best Practices While Writing

✅ Use main keyword in title
✅ Write natural, readable content
✅ Break up text with headers (H2, H3)
✅ Add images every 2-3 paragraphs
✅ Link to related content
✅ Write 800+ words for important posts
✅ Use short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)
✅ Make descriptions 120-160 characters

## Settings You Can Customize

Go to: **SEO Recommender → Settings**

- **Minimum Title Length** (default: 30 chars)
- **Minimum Description Length** (default: 120 chars)
- **Minimum Content Length** (default: 300 words)

Adjust these based on your needs!
